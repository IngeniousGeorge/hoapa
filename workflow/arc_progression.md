# Arc Progression

This file is a compacted progression of the numbered arc files under
`workflow/deep_context/arc`.
It is not a consensus-only summary.
It preserves the movement of the conversation: conjectures, false starts,
corrections, durable residues, and current settlements.
The goal is to let a new agent enter the project's dynamics rather than
inherit a frozen doctrine.
When a later section corrects an earlier section, the earlier section is
still recorded.
That is intentional.
Some early ideas became code.
Some early ideas became guardrails.
Some early ideas were retired as anchors but remained useful as metaphors,
heuristics, or future long-horizon targets.
The current short form is pair-first:
`Allow ⊣ Require` is the central cross-site adjunction.
The four large readings called Complexity, Evolution, Praxis, and Anthropic
are now best treated as faces of that one adjunction, not as four earned
functors.
Care is held orthogonally as an order-2 involutive structure, with session
duality, attachment, and boundary reversal as its B-side signs.
That current settlement is the endpoint of this document, not the rule by
which earlier turns are silently erased.

## 0. The Top-Level Frame Before The Arc

The top-level workflow files give the current state that the arc must be
read against.
`workflow/origins.md` roots the project in a practical frustration from
turn-based game communities and collaborative software.
The initial operational problem was not abstract mathematics.
It was that communities can coordinate for a while, then lose shared memory,
protocol discipline, role clarity, or auditable continuity.
Participants should be unityped at the protocol level.
Humans, LLMs, completed conversations, and other agents should not become
different kernel species.
What varies should be typed data, typed sessions, roles, interfaces, and
protocol state.
The "unityped agent / typed data" split is already present before the formal
arc starts.
The origin also imports a software-architecture motive.
Neal Ford and Mark Richards-style separation of concerns is not merely an
engineering preference.
It becomes the first recurring primitive: complexity becomes manageable when
layers do not smuggle each other's distinctions.
The origins also contain a social-political motive.
Authority should become less personal and more protocol-auditable above a
certain complexity threshold.
That is not a claim that judgment can be eliminated.
It is a claim that repeatable maintenance should move from charisma into
typed, inspectable, revisable processes.
`workflow/motivation.md` states the large conjectural axis.
Modal HoTT lets complexity be represented without collapsing it.
Typed data remains robust.
Unityped agents can handle complexity because agent identity is not where
the distinctions live.
The first slogan is the coupling of requires and allows.
A structure allows behavior by making it expressible.
A structure requires behavior by making continuation dependent on it.
That duality later becomes the cross-site `Allow ⊣ Require` pair.
The motivation file also records the expected cascade.
The Hybrid layer should mediate actor-locality and session content.
The Reflexive Protocol layer Π should mediate protocol versions and
self-modification.
The Bulk Content layer Λ should mediate generated languages, concepts, and
type theories.
Field-theoretic dynamics should eventually describe how content and protocol
states flow, stabilize, and deform.
Federation should prevent one protocol from becoming the only protocol.
Information cohomology or receiver-relative compression should eventually
explain why complexity is relative to the receiving system.
`workflow/related_structures.md` records families of resemblance.
Connes-style noncommutative geometry and modular flow remain loose
analogies.
Bennequin, Belfiore, Laforgue, and Huawei-like topos cognition are closer
family resemblances.
A5, icosahedral symmetry, dessins, and Belyi maps are detection heuristics
and possible long-horizon witnesses, not current foundations.
Cybernetics, Peirce, Pask, Maturana and Varela, Luhmann, Friston, HoTT,
univalent foundations, Bourbaki, Chu spaces, formal concept analysis, and
Barwise-Seligman information flow all appear as precedent fields.
The file's discipline is not to treat resemblance as proof.
The project should borrow structure only when the local formal problem earns
it.
`workflow/arc.md` is the current condensed form.
It is pair-first.
Modal HoTT is the A side.
Concurrency is the B side.
`Allow : M → K` is the left, generative direction.
`Require : K → M` is the right, reflective direction.
The pair encodes a circuit between modal complexity and operational
interaction.
The five current anchors are Complexity, Evolution, Praxis, Anthropic, and
Care.
Complexity is associated to `Allow`, the left/generative move from modal
structure into operational possibility.
Evolution is associated to `Require`, the right/reflective move from
operational structure back into modal constraints.
Praxis is associated to the unit `η : id_M ⇒ Require ∘ Allow`.
Anthropic is associated to the counit `ε : Allow ∘ Require ⇒ id_K`.
Care is associated to an order-2 involution, orthogonal to the adjunction.
The current `arc.md` also warns that triples, quadruples, ambidexterity, and
cohesive analogies are future extras unless earned.
They are not the base contract.
`workflow/status.md` gives the code-facing state.
The B-side has moved from A5/icosahedral speculation to an actor-causal and
Caires-Pfenning-inspired hybrid.
That hybrid is then augmented by the O1 interaction algebra.
Actors/locality live in the site.
Session types and duality live in the value/content algebra.
O1 supplies interaction shape, not the whole world.
Finite descent, finite sheaf categories, finite coverage, SET comparison,
and a conditional Benton/LNL sheaf bridge have landed.
The next active frontier is the finite sheafification reflector.
The current track says that a bankable publishable target is the finite
sheafification reflector and the unconditional sheaf-level Benton bridge
that it would enable.
The fallback targets are a non-collapse theorem for `Require ∘ Allow` or a
Lawvere-style asymmetry theorem.
The recommendation is to pursue the finite sheafification reflector first.
`workflow/future_work.md` keeps the debt honest.
The modal fracture story is still an over-approximation.
The concrete bar/cyclic recursion story is not yet implemented.
Sessions have an O1 action scaffold but not the full Caires-Pfenning
derivation.
The actor-causal site has finite and pullback infrastructure, but full
general descent and richer operational morphisms remain future work.
Chu duality is deferred, not rejected.
Differential cohesion and the de Rham ladder are future modal-side work.
The Elixir runtime, Agda trace kernel, and Pijul-shaped persistence layer
remain implementation targets.
Π, Λ, field-theoretic dynamics, Federation, and information cohomology are
future layers, not present code.
`workflow/bias_correction.md` is the negative anchor.
It says not to confuse modal HoTT with plain HoTT.
It says not to make the site a circle or Bℤ when that belongs to the modal
presentation.
It says not to treat concurrency as decorative.
It says not to pretend synthetic cohesion alone solves the B-side.
It says not to claim full event-dependent sheaf content is done.
It says not to collapse Set-valued and V-valued sheaves.
It says not to treat R4 as complete before the reflector exists.
It says not to infer a hidden morphism `𝟙ₛ ↔ ⊥ₛ`.
It says every warm word must hang from a structural fact.
`workflow/naming.md` fixes terminology.
The cohesive names are `flat ♭`, `sharp ♯`, and `shape ʃ`.
The cross-site names are `Allow`, `Mediate`, and `Require`.
`Mediate` is deferred or identity-shaped when the pair-only form is being
used.
`workflow/elixir.md` prevents premature runtime work.
The current Agda is close to a scaffold, but a trace-checker-facing protocol

The proposed Agda protocol kernel includes trace, session step, restriction,
auditability, and certificate modules.
The runtime should be Elixir/Erlang-shaped, the specification Agda-shaped,

It should not begin as a Pijul fork or plugin.
`workflow/targets.md` names T1 through T7 plus `T_recursion`.
T1 is the cross-site pair.
T3 is Lawvere asymmetry or non-collapse.
`T_recursion` is the iterable unit and developing network direction.
The targets file also keeps the B operational anchor and Sketch-of-Program

This top-level frame matters because the raw arc conversation often explores

The compacted arc should preserve that exploration while marking what is

## 1. The First Arc: From Galois To Adjunction

The first arc file begins with a search for the right mathematical shape.
One early candidate was a Galois connection.
That made sense because the project was already thinking in terms of two
worlds and a tension between them.
But the conversation quickly moved toward adjoint modalities rather than a
bare Galois story.
The reason was that modalities can encode structure, reflection,
coreflection, and internal movement in a way a simple order-theoretic
connection cannot.
Reflective and coreflective subcategories became the first precise mental
model.
A reflective subcategory receives a best approximation from the outside.
A coreflective subcategory sends a best expansion or presentation outward.
This already resembled "typed data" and "unityped agents" without yet
having the later B-side formalism.
At this stage the A side was more developed than the B side.
The A side was cohesive or modal HoTT.
The B side was still a pressure, not yet an operational site.
The conversation noticed a hidden partition inside A.
The cohesive triple `shape / flat / sharp` could be read as a partition
between typed data, unityped agents, and protocol shape.
That was not yet the final B-side, but it gave the first clue that the
project needed more than one kind of modality.
The engineering protocol supplied a concrete version.
The system should know only one way to interact with agents.
It should not branch on whether an agent is human, an LLM, a completed
conversation, or something else.
The distinctions should appear in data, roles, sessions, payloads, and
protocol state.
This was the first seed of the later `ActorBase` discipline.
The first arc considered several formal encodings.
Maybe the protocol is a Lawvere theory.
Maybe it is a modality.
Maybe it is an operational reading of cohesion.
Maybe it is a site whose covers represent perspective and compatibility.
No single encoding won immediately.
That uncertainty is important because the project was not yet trying to
prove a theorem.
It was trying to find the shape of the theorem worth proving.
The A5 and icosahedral branch appears early.
The idea was that A5 symmetry, 12-ary structure, or icosahedral geometry
could witness a finite, nontrivial, highly symmetric obstruction.
This was attractive because it connected finite formalization,
Grothendieckian dessins, and no-global-frame behavior.
A5 also suggested a way to make "perspective" mathematical without making
it psychological.
The early promise was an equivariant cohesion theorem.
There might be a finite symmetry whose fixed-point behavior forces a
distinction between local and global views.
There might be a way to connect that to no global section, no global frame,
or no privileged viewpoint.
This became a productive but eventually retired anchor.
It should not be erased because several motifs survived it.
Finite witnesses survived.
No-global-frame reasoning survived.
Dessins and Belyi maps survived as long-horizon language.
The search for a small formal non-collapse witness survived.
The use of A5 as the actual B-side operational anchor did not survive.
The first arc also placed B above A in an adjunction-like posture.
The conversation imagined B as praxis, operational protocol, or social
realization over the A-side foundations.
This was not yet the later actor-causal site.
It was more like the intuition that modal foundations and practical
protocols mutually constitute one another.
Grothendieck supplied a narrative map.
`Pursuing Stacks` was read as A.
`Esquisse d'un Programme` was read as B.
`Récoltes et Semailles` was read as praxis or the historical wound that
forces the math to matter.
The 3-pointed sphere, dessins, Belyi maps, and A5 appeared as possible
finite witnesses of that Grothendieck program.
This is one of the earliest forms of the project's ambition:
make a small formal artifact that carries a large historical and conceptual
charge.
The first arc therefore has two layers.
One layer is the still-valid shift from Galois-like duality to adjoint
modal structure.
The other layer is the exploratory A5/dessin anchor, now retired as a
foundation but retained as an intuition pump and future witness family.
The correction that comes later is not "the first arc was wrong."
The correction is that the first arc did not yet have an operational B-side.
It had an A-side, a pressure, and a set of beautiful finite metaphors.
The later work keeps the pressure and replaces the B anchor.

## 2. The Second Arc: B Needs Its Own Operational Anchor

The second arc is a major correction.
The conversation notices that B cannot merely be cohesion repeated on the
other side.
B needs its own operational anchor beneath any later cohesive reading.
The first formulation was too tempted to ask "what is the B-side cohesion?"
The correction says to ask "what is the B-side operational site?"
Only after that should one ask whether a cohesion-like structure emerges.
This is the moment where concurrency stops being decorative.
It must not be a metaphor attached to modal HoTT.
It must be a real operational domain with its own primitives.
Several candidates were considered.
HYC-style multiparty sessions offered roles, projection, and protocol
discipline.
Asynchronous π-calculus offered a standard process-calculus substrate.
Caires-Pfenning propositions-as-sessions offered a linear-logical session
type interpretation.
Winskel, Goguen, and Ghrist supplied presheaf, sheaf, and topos bridges
from concurrency into mathematics.
Cubical Agda was considered as a shared implementation substrate.
But Cubical was not allowed to become the B-site.
The B-site should be operationally meaningful, not just convenient for
formalization.
This distinction matters throughout the project.
Cubical Agda can host the proof.
It should not be confused with the thing proved.
The conversation then split into algebra-first and topology-first
possibilities.
Caires-Pfenning gives a clean algebra of sessions and duality.
But by itself it does not give locality, perspective, or sheaf-like gluing.
HYC-style roles and projections give perspective.
But by themselves they do not fully settle the mathematical topology.
The hybrid solution emerges from this tension.
Use an actor-causal, GR-inspired base topology for locality.
Use Caires-Pfenning-style session values as typed content over that base.
Separate the base from the values.
Separate actors from sessions.
Separate locality from payload.
Separate operational occurrence from typed message content.
The B-side then becomes a sheaf-theoretic semantics for actor-based session
types.
This is one of the project's decisive moves.
Actors and events form the locality leg.
Session types and duality form the content leg.
Covers express partial local views.
Descent expresses compatible local conversations gluing to coherent shared
context.
This is the first mature form of "separation of concerns" on B.
It is not just a coding style.
It becomes the definition of the site.
Actors live in the base.
Events touch actors.
Events are causally ordered.
Typed content is not in the actor record.
Roles are not actor kinds.
Payload is not actor identity.
Promotion is not a hidden constructor of actors.
Lamport partial orders become the minimal causal commitment.
The project deliberately does not commit yet to Lorentzian geometry,
Petri nets, richer event structures, or full GR.
Those remain refinements.
The B-side should be publishable on its own.
The idea is a sheaf-theoretic semantics for actor-based session types,
formalized in Cubical Agda.
This B-side publishability becomes important later because it disciplines
the whole project.
If the cross-site grand story remains too large, the B-side still yields a
bankable mathematical artifact.
The correction also changes how the first arc is read.
A5 and dessins are no longer the B anchor.
Cohesion is no longer the immediate B answer.
The operational B anchor is actor-causal plus session-valued sheaves.
Only after that can O1, MO1, cyclic structure, or field-theoretic dynamics
be layered responsibly.

## 3. Attachment, T_recursion, O1, And The Spherical Temptation

The third arc is the largest early expansion.
It begins with external conversations and a sense that something is missing.
The missing item is named `T_recursion`.
`T_recursion` is not just another theorem label.
It is the iterable unit of an adjunction.
It is the mechanism by which a system can repeatedly pass through its
protocol/environment circuit and generate a developing network.
It is connected to cyclic or modular operads.
It is connected to the strange "1 = 2" intuition where the cared-for and
caregiver can swap positions.
It is connected to the idea that a conversation can become a participant in
a later conversation.
It is also connected to scale.
The conversation discusses CFTs and fractal-like behavior.
The correction is that `T_recursion` is not itself a CFT and not itself a
fractal.
It is a possible substrate for CFT-like or fractal-like scale behavior.
The important claim is about recursive self-similarity through protocol
iteration, not about importing a finished physics theory too early.
The A-side spherical diagram was corrected during this arc.
The route is not simply `B²ℤ`.
The route is Bℤ as a site or presentation, then S¹ by a shape/presentation
switch, then S² by suspension.
That distinction matters because the modal side should not silently confuse
classifying types, shapes, and suspensions.
The B-side spherical route appears through O1 and MO1.
O1 is introduced as a cyclic operad with one binary operation and an
involution-like boundary behavior.
MO1 is the modular envelope.
Evaluation at `(0,3)` points toward a sphere with three marked points, the
home of trinion and dessin intuitions.
At this stage the conversation is tempted by a beautiful alignment:
A-side Bℤ/S¹/S².
B-side O1/MO1/S² with three marks.
Care as boundary swap.
Attachment as a structural operation.
The later correction does not destroy this alignment.
It postpones it.
O1 should augment the hybrid as an interaction algebra.
O1 should not replace the actor-causal site or the session-valued sheaf
substrate.
MO1 is a long-horizon stage.
The hybrid must remain stable underneath it.
This is one of the key lessons of the third arc:
operadic beauty is not allowed to eat locality.
O1 has three initial structural matches.
First, one binary operation matches a primitive actor interaction.
Second, boundary swap suggests session duality and care's "1 = 2" motion.
Third, three ports suggest sender, receiver, and channel, or a trinion-like
interface.
The operadic candidate was refined.
Commutative cyclic structure was considered.
Associative cyclic structure was preferred.
`Com_cyc` is too symmetric for actor/session order and ribbon/dessin
behavior.
`Ass_cyc` keeps cyclic order, which better matches sessions, ribbon
surfaces, ordered interaction, and dessins.
The name later stabilizes as O1 or cyclic associative O1.
This arc also links O1 to a care essay.
Care is not a moral property of agents in the kernel.
Care is a primitive operation of maintaining, continuing, repairing, or
holding a relation across a boundary.
Because agents are unityped, the giver and receiver of care can swap.
The typed protocol records the difference.
The actor kind does not.
This makes care structural rather than sentimental.
The "1 = 2" intuition belongs here.
It does not mean arithmetic collapses.
It means an operation can identify two roles through a reversible boundary
or duality movement.
The cared-for can become caregiver.
A completed conversation can become a participant.
The receiver can become sender under duality.
The boundary can be read from the other side.
The arc also asks why separation of concerns is optimal.
Several answers are tried.
There is a Shannon-like coding answer: systems compress better when stable
interfaces isolate variation.
There is a categorical answer: good abstractions satisfy universal
properties and are therefore optimal relative to a mapping problem.
There is a dynamical answer: separated layers make systems more resilient
under change.
The project does not settle on a metric notion of optimality.
It keeps the universal-property notion as the safest mathematical target.
This arc includes an operadic inventory.
It asks how far O1 can go.
It asks when MO1 becomes necessary.
It asks whether cyclic bar constructions are needed on A.
It asks whether Chu duality or bar/cobar-like machinery should be pulled in.
The later answer is conservative.
Start O1 as a stepping stone.
Keep MO1 as long horizon.
Keep the hybrid stable.
Do not skip finite descent and R4.
Build the bar/S¹ story on A only when the current modal scaffolding can
support it.
Chu remains deferred.
The third arc also contains an external observer critique.
The project is ambitious.
It risks endless scope expansion.
It needs to ship B-side content.
This critique becomes more important later than some of the speculative
content.
It is one reason the current track focuses on finite sheafification and a
bankable sheaf-level bridge rather than trying to solve the whole
modal/concurrency/federation universe at once.
The third arc therefore widens the vision and simultaneously creates the
need for stricter guardrails.
Its durable contributions are `T_recursion`, O1, Ass_cyc, the
S¹/S² correction, care as boundary operation, and the warning that O1 must
not replace the hybrid.

## 4. Care Above The Complexity Threshold

The fourth arc is short but important.
It introduces Caramello-style topoi as bridge-machines.
The idea is that topoi can translate between theories, sites, and semantic
worlds.
This aligns with the project because the project keeps needing bridges
rather than a single monolithic foundation.
The arc also sharpens the "complexity threshold" intuition.
A is the threshold of complexity.
B is care.
Evolution is described provocatively as "what infinity wants."
That phrase is dangerous if read teleologically.
The correction is immediate.
It should be read structurally, not as cosmic intention.
Complexity crosses a threshold when persistence depends on coherent
interaction among perspectives.
At that point, care becomes structurally necessary.
Care means maintenance of compatibility, continuation, repair, and
auditable relation.
It is not an optional sentiment added after formalization.
It is what a complex distributed system must do if it is to remain itself
through local differences.
The durable sentence from this arc is:
whenever complexity crosses the point where persistence depends on coherent
interaction among perspectives, care becomes a structural necessity.
This is a bridge between the warm language and the formal core.
Care must be backed by covers, restrictions, duality, descent, session
discipline, and eventually protocol revision.
If those structures are absent, "care" is only rhetoric.

## 5. Program Verification As A Motivating Concrete Target

The fifth arc grounds the mathematics in implementation.
Agda is not supposed to verify human wisdom.
Agda is not supposed to decide whether a conversation is good.
Agda can verify protocol-kernel traces.
That means well-formedness of events.
It means valid session steps.
It means valid promotion, when promotion exists.
It means no actor/session leakage.
It means provenance is preserved.
It means dual endpoints line up.
It means auditability has a formal core.
This is the practical meaning of "motivated program verification."
The architecture is split.
Agda holds the specification.
Elixir/Erlang can hold the runtime.
Event logs record what happened.
A checker verifies traces.
Rust may later provide a small high-performance core if needed.
The point is not to make the whole runtime dependently typed.
The point is to make the kernel contract explicit enough that runtime
behavior can be audited against it.
This arc also connects open source governance to the formal project.
Above the complexity threshold, authority should not remain only personal.
Maintainers remain people with judgment, but the project should push
repeated governance operations into typed, auditable protocols.
Maintainer status becomes a role/session/process rather than an essence of
a person.
This directly echoes the unityped-agent discipline.
The actor should not reveal whether it is a person, LLM, or conversation.
The protocol should reveal which role, capability, endpoint, provenance, or
authority it currently carries.
The arc also defends Cubical Agda as the current proof substrate.
The reason is not that Lean is bad.
The reason is that the project is HoTT/modal/HIT/path-native.
Cubical Agda is closer to that substrate.
Lean's classical ecosystem may be better for many other tasks, but this
project's core objects are modal, cohesive, path-sensitive, and
higher-inductive.
This arc is important because it stops the project from remaining only a
philosophical system.
The intended artifact is a protocol kernel that can eventually check real
conversation-of-conversations traces.
The current code is not there yet.
But the Agda modules are shaped to keep that path open.

## 6. The Hybrid As Adapter Discipline And Door To Field Theories

The sixth arc is another major correction and consolidation.
It insists on the distinction between O1 and the Hybrid.
O1 is protocol shape.
O1 is the grammar of interaction.
The Hybrid is the world in which interactions are located and typed.
The Hybrid consists of actor-causal locality plus session-valued sheaves.
If O1 replaces the Hybrid, the project loses locality, covers, and descent.
If the Hybrid ignores O1, the project loses cyclic interaction shape.
They are complementary layers.
The presheaf-to-sheaf interpretation becomes clearer here.
A local actor-causal frame carries typed content.
Restriction maps content along subviews.
Separatedness means two global sections that look the same locally are the
same globally.
Descent means compatible local sections glue to a global section.
Sheafification means forcing compatible local documentation into coherent
global context.
The auditability reading becomes precise.
Separatedness is non-equivocation.
If every local view agrees, there is no hidden global semantic difference.
The project chooses a safe form of separatedness.
It quantifies over arbitrary probes `X`.
It does not hard-code global sections as maps out of `𝟙ₛ`.
This matters because in a linear or *-autonomous setting the tensor unit is
not automatically a terminal object.
The sixth arc also corrects a tempting unit/dualizing collapse.
The tensor unit `𝟙ₛ` and the dualizing object `⊥ₛ` are distinct readings.
There is no hidden canonical conversion `𝟙ₛ ↔ ⊥ₛ`.
If a mix-like bridge is added later, it must be explicit.
The code now follows this discipline by providing both constant presheaves
and no silent morphism between them.
The Hybrid is reframed as a list of adapters.
Adapter 1 is Restriction.
A sub-view on the actor side induces a restriction of typed content on the
value side.
Adapter 2 is Descent/Gluing.
Compatible local typed content should glue to global typed content.
Adapter 3 is Unit/Dualizing Bridge.
Any bridge between `𝟙ₛ` and `⊥ₛ` must be a named additional structure.
Adapter 4 is Operadic Valuation.
O1 algebra should lift into presheaves valued in O1-algebras.
Adapter 5 is Promotion.
A completed conversation can become an actor or protocol-level participant,
but not through an ordinary `FrameHom`.
Adapter 6 is `Allow ⊣ Require`.
This is the cross-site modal adapter between A and B.
Adapter 7 is No-Leak/Erasure.
Session payloads, roles, and types must not refine actor identity.
This adapter list becomes a major guide for later code.
The arc develops a cobordism/session dictionary.
A boundary corresponds to a session interface.
An opposite boundary corresponds to a dual session.
A bordism corresponds to a process.
Gluing corresponds to cut.
Evaluation and coevaluation correspond to opening and closing interaction.
Trace and feedback correspond to recursive process structure.
This dictionary is not yet a theorem.
It is a disciplined source of future field-theoretic structure.
The arc also develops delocalization and relocalization.
Forgetting an actor-site should not mean throwing information away.
It means extracting session content from one actor-local presentation and
placing it over another.
The original actor site survives as shape, provenance, and boundary data.
This is a key phrase:
the category should be capable of turning local interaction into global
meaning, and global meaning back into new local possibilities.
The Sketch-of-Program mapping becomes less arbitrary here.
The site gives locality.
The value category gives typed content.
Covers and descent give gluing.
Generators and operations give protocol shape.
Recursive enlargement gives developing networks.
Comparison functors give cross-site structure.
Pijul enters as an implementation-facing analogy.
A patch or change resembles a typed event.
A dependency resembles causal order.
Commutation resembles independence.
A conflict resembles a gluing obstruction.
A partial clone resembles a local view.
But the arc warns not to approach Pijul maintainers prematurely.
The project needs a technical artifact first.
This arc also collapses actor kinds into `Participant`.
Roles should live at the session layer.
Capabilities should live at the protocol layer.
Endpoints should live in typed content.
Actor identity should remain opaque.
This directly anticipates `ActorBase.agda`.
The tensor unit becomes a second field-theory anchor.
It can represent empty boundary, closed protocol interface, or scalar
content.
But again, `𝟙ₛ` and `⊥ₛ` are not silently equated.
The sixth arc is therefore the discipline chapter.
It lets the field-theory door open without letting physics metaphors
overwrite the code-facing architecture.

## 7. Self-Modifying Protocol And The Π Layer

The seventh arc begins with a caution.
The opening overclaims.
It treats self-scanning too quickly as if the current `η`-iteration
already captured it.
The correction says no.
The current Agda iteration scaffold collapses in the cohesive sanity
instance.
Self-scanning is an operational interpretation of a future cross-site
recursion or bar construction.
It is not present in the current modal code.
This file is a model for how `arc_progression.md` should behave.
It keeps the overread.
It records the correction.
It then extracts a future layer.
The layer is Π, the protocol-version category.
Protocols are objects of Π.
Protocol edits are morphisms in Π.
Ordinary conversations are indexed by the active protocol object.
A meta-conversation can inspect the current protocol.
A closed meta-conversation can propose or emit a protocol edit.
Future events use the new protocol.
Past events are not retroactively retyped.
Non-retroactivity is central.
The protocol can modify itself, but the history remains auditable under the
protocol versions active at the time.
This matches the implementation need for event logs and certificates.
Consensus appears as future colimit-like structure.
The idea is that multiple edit proposals form a diagram.
A coherent amalgamation or colimit produces the next protocol version.
Pijul is the primary implementation-facing candidate for patch structure.
But the mathematics is not yet settled.
The fragment formalism could be a category.
It could be a double category.
It could be an event structure.
It could be adhesive or partial-commutation based.
The arc refuses to pick too early.
The current code anticipates this layer only by reservation.
`FrameHom` has strict causality reflection, so promotion cannot be a
sub-view embedding.
`Sessions.agda` defers conversation-of-conversations.
`Hybrid.Presheaf.agda` names Promotion as an adapter.
But there is no `Hybrid/Reflexive.agda` implementation yet.
There is no protocol-version object.
There is no edit morphism.
There is no version-indexed conversation record.
MO1 becomes more load-bearing in this arc.
The modular envelope is the natural place where completed conversations can
become higher-level participants or operations.
But the arc explicitly does not let MO1 skip descent or R4.
The order is:
stabilize Hybrid and finite descent;
stabilize the sheaf-level bridge;
then open the reflexive protocol layer when the fragment formalism is
honest enough to state.
The durable content of this arc is Π, non-retroactive protocol edits,
meta-conversations as diagrams of patches, and the distinction between
anticipated future recursion and current code.

## 8. The Bulk Content Layer Λ

The eighth arc opens another large layer.
The protocol does not merely route messages.
It generates language.
It generates concepts.
It generates refinements.
It generates equivalences.
It generates the type theory by which future conversations classify their
content.
This is the Λ layer.
Messages need a "how" tag and a "what" tag.
The "how" tag belongs to session/protocol structure.
The "what" tag belongs to content/language structure.
Earlier layers can describe who may speak, what endpoint they hold, and how
the interaction proceeds.
Λ asks what conceptual object is being produced by that interaction.
One high-altitude proposal treats Λ as a concept category or
2/(∞,1)-category.
Sessions might be paired with concepts as something like
`Sessions-Cat × C`.
Or the content layer might be a fibration over protocol states.
Seed languages could be initial presentations.
Language generation could be a functor, monad, or higher process.
Bias correction could be homotopical obstruction.
The conservative correction is simpler.
Λ is a category of language states, theories, signatures, or type theories.
Objects are languages or theories.
Morphisms are extensions, refinements, quotients, translations, or
identifications.
Conversations produce diagrams in Λ.
Consensus produces amalgamations.
This parallels Π but is distinct from it.
Π modifies protocols.
Λ modifies generated content.
The same conversation may affect both, but they should not be collapsed.
The arc then introduces field-theoretic content dynamics.
Content may behave like a factorization algebra over the actor-causal site.
Local conceptual insertions combine over disjoint or compatible regions.
Renormalization-group-like flow may describe how local details wash out
into stable effective concepts.
Mature language may be an infrared fixed point.
Different communities may land in universality classes.
Meta-conversations may act like deformations or insertions.
This is again a disciplined analogy, not code.
The practical import is that the project may eventually need action
functionals, metrics, and observables.
Metrics should apply to concepts, protocol versions, or language states,
not to actor essences.
Correlators might measure how content in one region affects content in
another.
The arc states a symmetric type/field slogan.
On the concurrency side, field-theory-shaped dynamics may stabilize into an
internal type theory.
On the modal side, type-theory-shaped foundations may have internal
semantics that are field-theory-shaped.
This is powerful but explicitly future-facing.
For the modal side to output field-theoretic structure, the project likely
needs differential cohesion.
It likely needs something like a de Rham or cohesive ladder.
It likely needs cyclic bar/S¹ machinery.
The current scaffolds do not yet do this.
The arc then reframes cohesion itself.
Plain cohesion is the floor at which the framework makes sense.
Differential cohesion is the floor at which the central conjecture may land.
Without differential cohesion, the modal side may not be rich enough to
support the field-theoretic reading.
Manin enters as a tentative relation.
Manin and Marcolli-like connections are plausible but not verified.
The arc warns not to cite that family as established alignment without
checking.
The eighth arc then generalizes recursion into context/system co-evolution.
Instead of a fixed system in a fixed context, the project considers pairs
`(M_n, C_n)`.
The system and context jointly generate the next system and context.
This is a formal frame for context-system co-evolution.
It is not yet a generalized theory of evolution.
That overclaim is explicitly rejected.
The project should avoid the cybernetics/GST/NKS failure mode where a very
general vocabulary explains everything and therefore proves nothing.
The eighth arc introduces Federation.
There should not be one true protocol.
Hoapa should be one protocol among many.
Protocols should interoperate, translate, and disagree.
An operational federation would need a neighbor registry.
It would need translation interfaces.
It would need provenance.
It would need partial clones.
It would treat conflict as data, not as failure to be hidden.
It would preserve edit-locality.
The mathematical version may be a category or bicategory of protocols.
Morphisms may be Hoapa-respecting geometric morphisms.
A Yoneda boundary `Hom(-, P)` may describe a protocol by how others map into
it.
A meta-topos of protocols is a long-horizon possibility.
Grothendieck-Teichmüller and anabelian geometry appear here as very distant
targets.
The idea is that an object can be reconstructed from its relations to all
others.
Dessins and protocol presentations re-enter through that door.
This is not current work.
It is a horizon that explains why early A5/dessin intuitions were not
random.
The eighth arc also develops receiver-relative complexity.
Complexity is not absolute.
It depends on the receiver, its language, its memory, and its reference
frame.
Possible mathematical tools include information cohomology, relative
entropy, conditional Kolmogorov complexity, MDL, and rate-distortion.
Claude emphasized Baudot-Bennequin-Vigneaux style entropy as a cocycle,
language formation as homology, and pairings like `H_* ⊗ H^* → k`.
Codex emphasized MDL, rate-distortion, compression, and operational
measurability more conservatively.
Both readings preserve the same structural point.
Differences in comprehension should live in receiver states and reference
frames, not in actor types.
This again supports unityping.
A practical proxy may be LLM perplexity or cross-entropy relative to a
given model/context.
That proxy is not the theorem.
It is an implementation handle on receiver-relative surprise.
The eighth arc is therefore the widening chapter.
It adds Λ, field dynamics, differential cohesion pressure, co-evolution,
federation, Yoneda/anabelian horizons, and information-cohomological
complexity.
Almost all of it is future work.
But it changes how the current B-side should be designed.
The current layers must leave room for language generation, protocol
revision, federation, and receiver-relative measurement.

## 9. The Quadruple Pressure And The Return To The Pair

The ninth arc starts by exploring a cross-site adjoint quadruple.
The four names are Complexity, Evolution, Praxis, and Anthropic.
The temptation is to make each into a functor.
One suggestion is two coupled adjunctions.
Another is a four-functor ladder analogous to cohesive quadruples.
Claude maps the four names to principles and directions.
The project briefly risks making the warm story mathematically primary.
Attachment and agape enter strongly here.
Attachment, care, and Peirce's agapism are read as micro and macro faces of
the same structural operation.
Tychasm becomes the B-side fluid proposal storm.
Anancasm becomes A-side rigid modal law.
Agapasm becomes adjunction or care.
Synechism becomes continuity, colimit, or the whole ambient H-like
continuum.
The anthropic illusion becomes a nominalist misreading corrected by
synechistic realism.
This Peircean mapping is suggestive.
It is not allowed to become the base formalism.
It remains a hermeneutic layer attached to structural facts.
The arc also uses cobordism duality.
Cups and caps resemble unit and counit.
Orientation reversal swaps cup and cap.
Duality and adjunction look unified in string diagrams.
This supports the feeling that care, boundary reversal, and adjunction are
not separate stories.
But the caveats are explicit.
Compact-closed, *-autonomous, open, and ribbon settings differ.
An involution is not a rotation.
Boundary reversal is not automatically O1 rotation.
The interpretation remains aspirational until MO1 and the relevant
categorical structure are actually built.
The conversation then evaluates the chiasmus critically.
The quadruple conflicts with the pair-shaped heuristic.
It also risks blurring T3, the Lawvere/non-collapse asymmetry.
Saying A and B are "the same ∞-topos" is too strong.
The safer reading is that A and B are subpresentations or related faces
inside an ambient H, not equivalent copies.
`Esquisse` should not be swallowed as merely "praxis."
It is geometry of B dynamics.
It is GT symmetry.
It is related to Λ output.
The public-story advice becomes strict.
Do not lead with a warm universal cybernetics-like story.
Do not present four faces as four functors before the math earns them.
Use attachment as an accessible front door if needed.
Use agape as a footnote or resonance, not as the load-bearing theorem.
Every warm word must hang off structural fact.
The formal pressure then collapses back to T3.
The real "if" is nondegeneracy.
A-side modal complexity and B-side separation must together make the unit
or monad iteration nontrivial.
If the unit collapses, `T_recursion` is only a scaffold.
If B-side separatedness/descent has no teeth, the operational side cannot
reflect anything interesting.
The optimality discussion is also disciplined.
Do not claim metric optimality without an objective.
Claim universal-property optimality when the construction actually gives
one.
The key correction is the table conflict.
One mapping put Praxis and Anthropic in positions that conflicted between
functors and unit/counit.
The conversation caught this.
The resolution is to stop forcing a quadruple of functors.
The current settlement is one adjoint pair with five anchors.
Complexity is `L` or `Allow : M → K`.
It is the generative left functor from modal structure into operational
possibility.
Evolution is `R` or `Require : K → M`.
It is the reflective right functor from operational behavior back into
modal constraint.
Praxis is the unit `η : id_M ⇒ R ∘ L`.
It is formally indexed on M.
It travels Modal → Concurrency → Modal.
It is felt in K as the practical generation of operational possibilities.
Anthropic is the counit `ε : L ∘ R ⇒ id_K`.
It is formally indexed on K.
It travels Concurrency → Modal → Concurrency.
It is felt as loss, approximation, or the impossibility of fully recovering
operational reality from modal reflection.
Care is the order-2 involution `_⊥`.
It is orthogonal to the adjunction.
It appears through session duality, boundary reversal, attachment, and the
caregiver/cared-for swap.
The arc calls this a conservation law.
If the adjunction is flipped, the intuitive locations of Praxis and
Anthropic become easier.
But flipping reverses the seed reading.
It would make community generate perspectives rather than modal complexity
allowing operational community.
It would also swap the Complexity/Evolution polarity.
The project keeps the seed reading.
The experiential/formal mismatch is documented rather than hidden.
Praxis is formally on M but felt in K.
Anthropic is formally on K but felt as modal loss.
That mismatch is itself part of the anthropic lesson.
The ninth arc is therefore the current resolution chapter.
Four faces remain useful.
They are not yet four functors.
The pair is load-bearing.
Care remains orthogonal.
Quadruple or ambidextrous structure may return later if the math earns it.

## 11. What Was Retired But Not Lost

A5 was retired as the operational B anchor.
It remains a finite-symmetry heuristic.
It remains connected to dessins, Belyi maps, no-global-frame behavior, and
possible small non-collapse witnesses.
The idea that B is simply cohesion-on-the-other-side was retired.
The residue is that B may later have cohesive or topos-like structure after
its operational site is built.
The immediate B answer is actor-causal/session sheaf structure.
The idea that O1 can replace the hybrid was retired.
The residue is that O1 is the interaction-shape algebra layered over the
hybrid.
The idea that MO1 can be opened immediately was retired.
The residue is that MO1 likely hosts promotion, conversation-of-conversations,
and modular-envelope structure later.
The idea that the current `η`-iteration already implements self-scanning
was retired.
The residue is that `η`-iteration is still the right scaffold for a future
developing-network theorem once the cross-site and cyclic/bar structures
exist.
The idea that `𝟙ₛ` and `⊥ₛ` can be silently bridged was retired.
The residue is that a named mix-like bridge may be added if the structure is
explicitly supplied.
The idea that Complexity, Evolution, Praxis, and Anthropic are four earned
functors was retired.
The residue is that they remain four useful readings of one adjunction.
The idea that Peirce, cybernetics, or warm care-language can lead the
formal exposition was retired.
The residue is that those languages can still interpret the structure after
the structure is named.
The idea that field theory is already present was retired.
The residue is a disciplined future path through cobordism/session
dictionaries, factorization algebras, differential cohesion, and RG-like
content dynamics.
The idea that federation can be skipped in favor of one true protocol was
rejected.
The residue is a future category or bicategory of protocols, translation
interfaces, provenance, partial clones, and conflict-as-data.
The idea that receiver-relative complexity can be represented by actor
kinds was rejected.
The residue is a future information-cohomological, MDL, or rate-distortion
reading where complexity lives in receiver state and reference frame.

## 12. Durable Concepts, In Current Order

The first durable concept is separation of concerns.
It starts as software architecture.
It becomes the unityped-agent/typed-data split.
It becomes actor locality versus session content.
It becomes O1 shape versus hybrid world.
It becomes protocol versions Π versus content languages Λ.
It becomes the whole adapter discipline.
The second durable concept is adjunction.
It starts as a move away from Galois-only thinking.
It becomes cohesion and modalities.
It becomes `Allow ⊣ Require`.
It becomes the LNL/Benton bridge inside B.
It becomes the unit/counit reading of Praxis and Anthropic.
The third durable concept is locality.
It starts as perspective and no-global-frame intuition.
It becomes actor-causal frames.
It becomes covers.
It becomes finite descent.
It becomes sheafification.
The fourth durable concept is operational B independence.
B must have its own primitives.
Concurrency is not decoration.
Actor-causal sites and session-valued sheaves are the current answer.
The fifth durable concept is cyclic interaction shape.
O1 supplies this.
It is associative cyclic rather than commutative cyclic.
It gives rotation, composition, and boundary-swap structure.
It awaits deeper algebraic connection to session duality.
The sixth durable concept is care.
Care is structural maintenance across complexity.
Care appears as boundary reversal, duality, attachment, repair, and
continuation.
Care is not a sentiment in the kernel.
Care is not an actor type.
Care is carried by typed operations and involutive structure.
The seventh durable concept is recursion.
`T_recursion` is the iterable unit and developing network path.
The current code has an iteration scaffold.
The nontrivial theorem is future.
The eighth durable concept is auditability.
Separatedness is non-equivocation.
Descent is compatible reconstruction.
Trace checking should verify protocol behavior without claiming to verify
human judgment.
The ninth durable concept is promotion.
A completed conversation can become a participant only through a future
promotion adapter.
It cannot be an ordinary `FrameHom`.
Promotion probably belongs near MO1 and Π.
The tenth durable concept is language generation.
Λ says conversations generate content languages and type theories.
Consensus can refine, quotient, extend, or translate content.
The eleventh durable concept is field dynamics.
The project expects local-to-global content dynamics to have field-like
structure.
This remains future and must pass through differential cohesion or
factorization-like machinery.
The twelfth durable concept is federation.
Protocols must compose, translate, fork, disagree, and preserve provenance.
Hoapa should not become the only protocol.
The thirteenth durable concept is receiver-relative complexity.
Complexity depends on receiver state.
It should not be encoded as actor kind.
Future information cohomology, MDL, rate-distortion, or cross-entropy tools
may make this measurable.
The fourteenth durable concept is non-collapse.
The project needs a theorem showing that the circuit does not degenerate.
T3 and the future cross-site `Require ∘ Allow` story are versions of this
need.
The fifteenth durable concept is finite publishability.
Large horizons are allowed.
But the current track should ship finite B-side theorems.
The finite sheafification reflector is the present bankable target.
