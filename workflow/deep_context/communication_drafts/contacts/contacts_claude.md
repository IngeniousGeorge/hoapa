# Hoapa — Contact map: Greater Helsinki academics

A working contact list of scholars in the Greater Helsinki region (University of
Helsinki, Aalto University, and a few adjacent institutions) whose work touches
the concepts Hoapa draws on. Compiled 2026-06-11 for outreach (cf.
`targeted_emails.md`, `email.md`).

## How to read this

- **Scope.** Greater Helsinki region, as agreed: primarily University of Helsinki
  (UH) and Aalto University, with the institution named per entry. A short
  appendix lists a handful of strongly-linked people *outside* the region for
  reference.
- **"Link" is deliberately loose.** Many entries are thematic neighbours, not
  people already working on this exact synthesis. That is intentional — the brief
  was to be liberal about what counts as a connection.
- **Emails.**
  - **✓ verified** — taken from the person's public research-portal profile
    (researchportal.helsinki.fi exposes the address).
  - **~ inferred** — constructed from the institutional pattern
    (`firstname.lastname@helsinki.fi` / `@aalto.fi`) and **not** individually
    verified. Aalto profiles hide the address behind a contact form, so **all
    Aalto emails are inferred.** Double-check inferred addresses before sending;
    a few UH staff use a middle initial or a non-standard local-part.
- **Links** go to the person's research-portal profile (UH:
  `researchportal.helsinki.fi`; Aalto: `research.aalto.fi`), which is the most
  stable public page and usually carries current contact details, publications,
  and CV links.
- Roles/seniority span professors to doctoral researchers (you asked to include
  everyone). Emeritus/visiting status is noted where known.

**Counts:** ~115 names total — Mathematics & Logic ~33, Philosophy ~17,
Engineering/CS ~30, Social Sciences ~33 (physicists folded into Math & Logic).
