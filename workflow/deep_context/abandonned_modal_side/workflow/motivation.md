# The Hoapa conjecture

*This note records Hoapa's mathematical conjecture, its metaphysical scaffolding, and the engineering target of the project. The math we're building here is a subsystem of a broader interdisciplinary picture describing open communities of inquiry. We believe it is, however, interesting to mathematics on its own merits.*

*Much of what follows can be traced to a single reframing: to pose foundational questions at the level of the community rather than the individual, following the observation that the world has no absolute perspective, and can therefore only be approached by a multiplicity. The focus shifts one step upstream in the scientific algorithm: from the structure of knowledge to the dynamics of inquiry that produce it.*

*To construct the protocols which answer those questions, one is forced into a specific architecture based on modularity—every part of the system must only do one thing. A key specification is for the individuals to all be treated in the same way by those systems—every participant in the system must play by the same rules.*

*To prove the Hoapa conjecture would be to demonstrate that this architecture is the optimal way to stay open. A generative openness that sustains itself without consuming what sustains it, and whose iteration begins the process again at a new scale.*

*Whether that recursion reaches all the way to a continuity across worlds is the metaphysical horizon the conjecture points to.*

## The foundational conjecture

Three facts are ordinary in their own domains:

- Modalities support the development of complexity (modalities, or perspectivism encoded in homotopy type theory).
- Typed data is more robust, readable, literate, and composable in any domain concerned with information transfer.
- Large numbers of unityped¹ agents are well adapted to handle complexity.

Based on those, the project targets a cross-site adjunction:

Allow ⊣ Require

between:

M = modal / contextual structure
K = concurrent / protocol structure

The modal side is the formal home of perspectivist context: the possibility that multiplicities emerge and develop complexity because there is no oppressive single privileged viewpoint. The concurrent side is the operational home of inquiry: unityped participants, typed communication, local traces, composability and generated documentation.

The conjecture says, in raw form:

If:

- K properly separates unityped actors from typed content;
- M supplies enough modal/perspectivist structure;
- the two sides are related by an Allow ⊣ Require adjunction;

Then:

- modal context allows concurrent complexity to unfold;
- concurrent praxis requires modal context to be legitimate and intelligible;
- iterations of the adjunction produce a developing network rather than collapsing;
- the protocol can generate typed documentation that becomes a new context for further inquiry.

The current pair-first anchoring is:

| Anchor | Adjunction datum | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Complexity | Allow = L : M → K | modal context generatively permits operational complexity |
| Evolution | Require = R : K → M | concurrent histories reflect back into modal structure |
| Praxis | η : Id(M) → R∘L | the universal opening through which particular operational work can be contextually presented |
| Anthropic-principle² loss | ε : L∘R → Id(K), especially non-invertibility | a community sees only a lossy reflection of other contexts; stronger non-collapse entails the relevant loss |
| Care | order-2 involution (–)⊥ | the checked syntactic proxy: receiver and giver, cared-for and caregiver, exchange roles; the operadic realization remains conditional |

For a particular operational episode `w : L M → K₀`, its contextual transpose is `w♯ = R(w) ∘ η_M : M → R K₀`. This resolves the apparent mismatch between Praxis being formally indexed on M and being experienced through work in K: `η_M` is the universal / synthetic opening, while `w` supplies the particular / analytic content. But this transpose exists for every adjunction, so the work–replay alignment clarifies the conjecture and supplies no evidence for this particular `Allow ⊣ Require`. The content would lie in constructing the pair and identifying a class of episodes whose transposes satisfy the required locality, controlled-loss, care, and non-collapse laws.

The anchor called Care also has two deliberately unequal statuses. The involution `(–)⊥` is the grounded structural proxy. Its interpretation as viability-preserving, non-coercive developmental renewal is a downstream theorem-shaper, not a redefinition; in particular, its carrier-conservation and anti-ranking requirements depend on the unproved 2S no-leak family. Likewise, Anthropic loss and persistent development are not independent evidence: their precise ordering is recorded once in the non-collapse ladder in `targets.md` §T3.

This is still a conjecture. The checked work so far is narrower: on the operational side, the project has a point-generated sheafification reflector and a sheaf-level Benton/LNL adjunction over the supported event-discrete actor-causal site. Chu(SET,Bool) supplies a concrete coefficient witness, and the Sessions-to-Chu realization interprets the postulate-free session object/morphism syntax. This is not yet the cross-site Allow ⊣ Require adjunction. It is one concrete fragment that makes the operational side less speculative.

¹: Unityping is the provision that the system which handles messages and their carriers can distinguish between types of messages, but can't distinguish between message carriers, who are all of the same type from the system's point of view, meaning the system treats all of them in exactly the same way—no privileges, no special responsibilities. This is, even if counter-intuitive to humans accustomed to a social landscape determined by specialization, a form of liberation for the message carriers, and a reclaiming of their own identity.

²: The Anthropic Principle is a partial solution to the fine-tunning puzzle: the observation that it looks like the universe "conspired" to create us (if you change some of the fundamental constants of physics by only a small amount, our universe either collapses or blows up). The Anthropic Principle says that the problem arises because we're around to talk about it, and we would not be here to notice a universe that wasn't. In the metaphysical framing suggested by the conjecture, this is further understood as a form of "optical illusion" due to the perspectivism baked into the fabric of the universe: all we see from distant contexts are the elements in those contexts that are compatible with ours, such that eventually, in the limit, it appears as though all those distant contexts did was prepare for our existence.

## Metaphysical framing: context, multiplicity, language-type, new context

*The metaphysical language below is not a theorem and not a condition required by the formal work, but it acts as the "provisional" context in which the conjecture can be meaningfully stated, to then become self-sufficient.*

**Compact formulation.** Reality unfolds through contexts that generate multiplicities; these multiplicities articulate their contexts through languages and type-structures: systems of constructions, expressions and transformations, of identity and difference. Once stabilized, such language-types become new contexts in which further multiplicities can emerge.

The metaphysical schema behind the project is:

```text
context → multiplicity → language/type → new context → new multiplicity → new language/type theory…
```

A context is a field of problems, perspectives, histories, constraints, and possible actions. Within it, multiplicities emerge: agents, events, local views, incompatible fragments, and paths of possible transformations. Those multiplicities fully enter a shared reality by producing a form of language, and more constructively by producing types: a system of forms in which what was locally encountered can be expressed, checked, inhabited, transported, revised, identified, composed and reused.

Logic already appears in this picture. It is a way of taking the resources that a situation makes available, working with them, and returning the result as a rule, type, document, or construction that can be adequately reused. (The next section makes that logical reading explicit.)

Hoapa does not try to prove this metaphysics. The conjecture would instead show that the schema has a precise communication-theoretic incarnation: a *perspectivist* context allows a *unityped* concurrent community to produce a *multityped* documentation, and the resulting documentation can become the seed of further context. This conjecture is in essence the bridge from the specification of the three adjectives in italics to an open logic for networks. The optimality, in the engineering sense, of those specifications helps the recursion in the schema above to happen.

A technical approach into this metaphysics sees it as a Hegelian sublation that stops short of his Absolute Idea with a deterritorialization of Deleuze's difference, via the mathematics of Lawvere. But more generally, one should conceive it as a nexus of traditions forming a meeting point of partial views on Hoapa, articulating what each provides without collapsing them into one doctrine. In the language of category theory, one may also imagine Hoapa as the colimit of an epistemological diagram.

| Neighbor | What it contributes | Absorbed here as |
|---|---|---|
| Hegel - Dialectic | dialectic; aufhebung; the concrete universal | each language-type sublates its predecessor — preserved, cancelled, lifted |
| Deleuze - Difference | multiplicity, difference, and non-final becoming | "multiplicity"; and the refusal to close (no first context, open colimit) |
| Lawvere - Cohesive topos | structured contexts have internal languages | the categorical spine of the schema |
| Peirce - Semiotics | sign, object, interpretant; inquiry as a community process | interpretation becoming habit, and habit becoming a new context |
| Lautman / Zalamea - Saturation and liberation | problematic structure, transit, saturation, and liberation | saturation and liberation, context completing enough structure to release new forms |
| Grothendieck - Relative point of view | relative point of view and rising-sea method | objects known through relations, covers, and patient enlargement of context |
| Whitehead - Process philosophy | reality as events and becoming rather than substance-first inventory | multiplicities forming through successive occasions |
| Simondon - Individuation | individuals precipitate from a metastable field | multiplicities resolving into typed forms without exhausting their field |
| Maturana / Varela - Autopoiesis | systems produce and maintain their own organization | language-types as self-maintaining disciplines of action |
| Cassirer / Goodman - Worldmaking | symbolic forms and world-versions | worldmaking as structural, not merely cultural |

In effect, this diagram serves as a reason for a community to act in the world with a methodology, specifically a formalization into 21st century mathematics and computer science of Peirce's "community of inquirers", together performing "agape". In our conjecture, the community is K, while Care is structurally represented by reciprocal role duality. Reading that involution as the developmental work of love or agape remains a conjectural adequacy claim, dependent in part on the no-leak family rather than established by the involution alone. The term is inspired by Bowlby's theory of attachment, whose "algorithm for network continuity", described in the [presentation of the project](../README.md), is a pillar of our research.

Once this concrete stage is reached, the metaphysical scaffolding becomes in a sense obsolete, and what matters is the testing of the actions of the community under the induced methodology. In terms inspired by Gentzen, the metaphysics is the "introduction rule", how one conceives the logical work, and the test is the "elimination rule", how one consumes the logical work.

## Logical interpretation: borrowing without collapse

**Compact formulation.** Logic is the theory of valid contextual transformation: the disciplined art of borrowing from a context without collapsing it, and returning what was borrowed as a reusable form of action, expression, or understanding.

In this setting, logic is not merely a calculus of inference. It is the practice of using context in a way that can be checked, shared, and repeated. A proof uses assumptions and turns the act into a rule that can be applied elsewhere. A type makes an operation viable. A sheaf uses local views and tests whether they agree enough to glue. A Hoapa protocol uses participants' perspectives and returns trusted, viable and suitably composed documentation without exposing, consuming, or ranking the participants.

There are simple real-world traces of the same pattern. Permaculture borrows from a place: sun, water, slope, soil, shade, succession, animal behavior, microbial cycles, and human labor. It succeeds when the borrowing strengthens the cycles that made the borrowing possible. A cell does something comparable with gradients: it uses a difference in its environment, transforms that difference into work, and preserves enough boundary and metabolism for future work to remain possible. In both cases, the logic is not domination of context but non-collapsing use of context.

This is why the logical interpretation is central to Hoapa. The project asks how a community can legitimately turn local constraints, observations, needs, and obligations into public, typed, revisable structure while preserving the openness of the context that made the transformation possible.

The logic-neighbor network is again not a dictionary of equivalences. It is a set of partial witnesses to the same problem: how context can be both used and preserved.

| Martin-Löf - Dependent type theory | propositions-as-types, proofs as inhabitants, identity as structure | the grammar of inhabiting and transforming a world |

## The cascade of trade-offs: a practical response to structural problems

The framework is, read operationally, a sequence of engineering trade-offs that the foundational commitments to the unityped/multityped discipline generate, together with the strategies that resolve them. The cascade is *generative*: each resolution opens the next problem. The strategy applied at every step is the same shape: separation of concerns, or modularity (each module must only do one thing, and complexity is handled at the level of compositionality). The full layer-by-layer catalogue is in `future_work.md`; what follows is the compact picture.

| Layer | Trade-off it navigates | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Hybrid** | Locality (actors operate in frames) vs globality (content must cohere across frames) | Sheaf descent: compatible local content glues to global |
| **Reflexive (Π)** | Stability (the protocol must be reliable) vs adaptability (it must respond to change) | Meta-conversations propose, consensus ratifies, non-retroactivity protects the past |
| **Bulk (Λ)** | Expressive power (rich vocabulary) vs transparency (decodable by every participant) | Protocol generates its own language; the reflexive layer maintains its transparency over time |
| **Field-theoretic dynamics** | Exploration (proposal instability, high entropy) vs exploitation (stable mature language, low entropy) | RG flow with reflexive insertions; symmetry-breaking → fluctuation → stabilization phases |
| **Federation** | Coherence within one protocol vs plurality across protocols | Typed translation interfaces, partial clones, conflict-as-data |
| **Information cohomology** | Dense encoding (expert-friendly) vs accessible encoding (decodable by any member of the unityped receiver population) | Receiver-relative measurement of encoding cost; transparency treated as an engineering property on the same footing as performance and security |

The cascade is not a list of features added by choice. It is the sequence of problems the foundational commitments create, with each layer being the structural response to the problem the previous layer's resolution exposed. Hybrid's locality-vs-globality only becomes a problem because we committed to separating actors and sessions. Reflexive's stability-vs-adaptability only becomes a problem because Hybrid gave us a maintainable artifact. Bulk's expressivity-vs-transparency only becomes a problem because Reflexive gave us a self-editing protocol that must now talk about things. Field-theoretic dynamics' exploration-vs-exploitation only becomes a problem because Bulk gave us a language that must evolve well. Federation's coherence-vs-plurality only becomes a problem because the four previous layers gave us a complete protocol that, in isolation, would re-create the one-true-framework failure the project was built to dissolve. Information cohomology's dense-vs-accessible encoding becomes salient as soon as Bulk produces a language, and acute as soon as Federation puts that language in front of multiple receiver populations.

The strategy is uniform across the cascade. Every trade-off is resolved by separating the two concerns that are in tension, giving each its own categorical home, and providing the precise interface between them. Locality and content are separated by the sheaf; current protocol and its edits are separated by a meta layer; how and what are separated by an orchestration/content split; content and dynamics are separated by the factorization-algebra-plus-RG-flow; interior and exterior of a protocol are separated by the meta-topos; content and its measurement are separated by the (co)homology pairing. The foundational commitments themselves (actor-vs-content on K, spatial-vs-discrete on M, context-vs-system at the cross-site adjunction) are the same architectural move at the foundational floor.

So separation of concerns is not an engineering principle the framework happens to use. It is the framework's primitive architectural move, applied recursively from the foundations upward, and the cascade of trade-offs is what makes that recursion necessary. The transparency-of-language trade-off (information cohomology) belongs on this list on the same footing as the others because it is the encoding question the unityped commitment forces on the engineer: *how is the unityped receiver going to understand the message?* That question is no less an engineering interest than performance, security, or maintainability. It is simply not currently on the engineer's radar, because the economy of expertise prefers density to transparency. In the framework it is how the output feeds back into the input (and what makes the loop go round, along with other aspects, as we shall see in the next section).

## Openness as the best strategy

The cascade does not just terminate in a finished protocol. It converges on a stance: an open community of inquiry, able to accept and process outside nodes (participants and concepts alike) as if they were its own without turning them into subordinates, threats, or ranked identities.

From the metaphysical/logical point of view, Hoapa is a strategy for borrowing from a context without collapsing it. It lets a community receive external perspectives and type them as stable documentation without consuming or exposing the participant-carriers that made the transformation possible, or distorting the concepts involved.

From the conjectural point of view, Hoapa seeks an optimal open strategy: a universal way for a community of inquiry to remain open under perspectivism. Here "optimal" is a system-architectural property: relative to an explicitly declared class of probes, observations, equivalences, and admissible open strategies, every such strategy should factor through the Hoapa construction in the stated sense. Neither the admissible class nor this universal property has yet been constructed. (It is not a hidden numerical metric—this layer may come later through information cohomology or field-theoretic dynamics, but the first meaning is given by category-theoretic universal properties.)

The current candidate network of notions is:

| Notion | Role |
|---|---|
| Trust | the expected behavior of an interface, from the point of view of its consumer (forward warrant) |
| Accountability | the reverse channel: the trace, answer, repair, or challenge path |
| Risk / vulnerability | the conserved exposure that makes trust nontrivial |
| Reliability | the settlement of expectation against delivered account |
| Legitimacy | earned standing relative to a problem, viewpoint, and admissible tests |
| Openness | the capacity to admit foreign profiles without erasing or subordinating them |
| Care | reciprocal role symmetry; its adequacy for continuity without extraction is conjectural and depends on the no-leak family |

This points toward a warrant-enriched network. Trust is an enriched hom: directional, contextual, and compositional, never a predicate on actors. Accountability is its audit or return channel. Legitimacy is representability or universal adequacy relative to a problem viewed a certain way; its grounded corners in the project are initiality and no-leak. Openness is a completion/admission discipline: a foreign node is accepted exactly when its two-sided profile toward the network is admissible.

```text
secure base → explore → compose findings → document → care → new secure base → new exploration → …
```

A *secure base*, in the attachment-theory sense, is a mature node carrying enough coherent, useful information to do its educational and developmental job.

In our metaphysics this dynamic also applies across contexts and multiplicities. It views field-theoretic physics as the secure base for chemistry, chemistry as the secure base for ecosystems, ecosystems as the secure base for natural-language-based sociological systems… At each step the borrowing-without-collapse logic and open strategies employed by natural systems allow the current multiplicity to develop and yield, as a byproduct, the next context, which can be a secure base for a new multiplicity to emerge.

Metaphorically, in the language of (co)homology, the stages chain like a complex: the image of one passage sits inside the kernel of the next, and the genuinely new at each level is the homology, exactly what the level below could not already account for.

In the conjecture, the same dynamic exists inside one developing network: a secure base is the reusable catalyst that lets a multiplicity freely explore, safe from exploitation. The base offers a field of engineering strategies to navigate, and the conjecture claims that the open strategy is the most adequate for continuity and persistence under the logic of borrowing-without-collapse. 

At the field-theoretic horizon, this reusable-catalyst face has the shape of a trace: closing the catalyst wire into a circle suggests the familiar identity ∫ over S¹ of R ≃ HH(R), Hochschild homology as the trace of repeated use. This is a different homology from the emergent one above: here it points to the trace of reuse, not novelty across levels. The two homologies correspond, respectively, to the conjectural and metaphysical readings of secure-base recursion. (This remains a metaphorical target for Hoapa, not an established result; and it describes the loop's cyclic continuity, not care itself. The checked proxy for Care is the order-2 involution inside the loop, the swap by which the cared-for can become caregiver. Showing that this swap preserves a viable, non-coercive cycle remains conjectural and depends on no-leak.)

The conjecture and the metaphysics are two points of view on why this kind of multiplicity-organization is adapted to its perspectivist context; *and/or* is the honest connective between them.
