Historical source snapshots, not live instructions. This directory preserves
the pre-edit text for the 1C reorientation on main. The spelling

## Decision and scope

The project no longer prescribes cohesive/modal HoTT as the world/context
setting or signature repair as the first 1C action. It seeks M through K's
categorical essence, alternatives, contextual adequacy, and renewal.
Cohesive mathematics remains reusable; relative cohesion is a new candidate
relationship, not the reinstatement of the old work order.

No Agda module is deleted, repaired, or moved by this migration. K's checked
results and the 2S/3R sequence retain their scope. The all-types-flat
diagnostic remains an unimplemented control; the analytic non-flatness
witness is already checked under its own definitions.

## Modal post-mortem

Unrestricted flat introduction plus dependent flat induction and the stated
β-rule make flat-counit an equivalence for every type. Thus the intended
flat-modal fragment becomes the whole universe. This is not a proof of
Booleanity, decidability of every observation, or omniscience, and it does not
invalidate cohesive mathematics generally. The synthetic and analytic
claims cannot jointly describe the intended same non-degenerate model.

The archived de Rham, differential, and sharp-modality discussions contain
historical conjectures and proposed encodings, not validated recipes. They
are preserved for provenance, not endorsed by inclusion here.
