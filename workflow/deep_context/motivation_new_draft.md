  ## Logical interpretation: borrowing without collapse

  Compact formulation. Logic is theory of valid contextual transformation: the disciplined art of borrowing from a context without collapsing it, and returning the borrowed dependency as a reusable transformation.

  In this setting, logic is not merely a calculus of valid inference. It is the normed passage by which a situated dependency becomes stable form. A proof borrows assumptions and
  discharges them. A type borrows a context and makes an operation inhabitable. A sheaf borrows local views and asks whether they glue. A protocol borrows participants' perspectives
  and returns documentation that can be reused without exposing or consuming the participants.

  This is why the logical interpretation is central to Hoapa. The project is not only asking what a community should believe. It asks how a community can transform contextual
  dependency into public, typed, revisable structure while preserving the openness of the context that made the transformation possible.

  The logic-neighbor network is again not a dictionary of equivalences. It is a set of partial witnesses to the same problem.

  The foundational conjecture

  Three facts, each trivial in its own domain:

  - [ModalHoTT] Modalities — perspectivalism encoded in HoTT — let complexity develop. (Trivial in abstract mathematics: Lawvere, Manin.)
  - [SessionValues] Typed data is more robust, readable, composable. (Trivial in any domain concerned with information transfer, such as programming.)
  - [ActorBase] Large numbers of unityped agents are best adapted to handle complexity. (Trivial in concurrency theory¹ — a lesson of the actor model.)

  The conjecture is that these facts are intimately linked, by an adjunction (encoded in category theory). If:

  - SessionValues and ActorBase are held apart by separation of concerns (typed data in the values, unityped agents in the base); and
  - ModalHoTT and the SessionValues–ActorBase pair stand in a requires–allows adjunction — the codependency that
    - perspectives require (for optimality) typed data handled by unityped ("univocal", in the Spinozist sense) agents;
    - perspectives allow typed data to be handled by unityped agents;
    - the separation of concerns between data and agents (the optimality it secures) requires perspectivalism — a modal network;
    - and allows it;

  then:

  - philosophically, the category hosting this structure is capable of world-understanding and world-creation (arc.md §7, held loose);
  - mathematically, something about covers/coverings, recursion, and contribution to a developing infinite network — the fragment interesting on its own (arc.md §6, targets.md 
  T_recursion).

  Current first checked fragment. On the operational side, the project has a point-generated sheafification reflector and a sheaf-level Benton/LNL adjunction over the supported
  event-discrete actor-causal site, with Chu(SET,Bool) as a concrete coefficient witness and the first Sessions-to-Chu J0/J1 realization. This is not yet the cross-site Allow ⊣ 
  Require; it is the first modest B-internal theorem plus value-model instance that makes one side of that future adjunction concrete.

  Current proof-near ethical fragment. The protocol protects participants not by recognising a formal predicate of humanness, but by refusing actor-origin predicates in the trusted
  kernel. The theorem-shaped core is no-leak / actor-renaming invariance: relabeling actors changes neither well-formedness, nor realized actor-causal structure, nor later trusted
  documentation observations. The fuller ethical reading stays motivation; the 2S target is the renaming theorem.

  The last item is the mathematical fragment — interesting for its own sake — of a foundational statement in the space of interdisciplinary theories.

    ## Openness as the best strategy

  The end result of the cascade can be read in two compatible ways.

  From the metaphysical/logical point of view, Hoapa is a strategy for borrowing from a context without collapsing it. It lets a community receive foreign perspectives, use them,
  type them, test them, and return them as stable documentation without consuming or exposing the participant-carriers that made the transformation possible.

  From the conjectural/categorical point of view, Hoapa seeks an optimal open strategy: a universal way for a community of inquiry to remain open under perspectivalism. Here
  "optimal" should first mean universal-property optimality, not a hidden numerical metric. The metric layer may come later through information cohomology or field-theoretic
  dynamics, but the first safe meaning is structural: the strategy is optimal if every admissible way of staying open factors through it.

  The current candidate network of notions is:

  In the mathematical direction suggested by arc/14, this points toward a warrant-enriched network: trust as an enriched hom, accountability as its dual channel, legitimacy as
  representability or universal adequacy, and openness as a completion/admission discipline. This is still a candidate target, not a theorem. Its value is that it gives the
  optimality claim something precise to aim at.

  The care loop is the same point in operational language. A secure base, in the attachment-theory sense, is not pre-given. It is produced by exploration, gluing, documentation, and
  care:

  explore -> glue -> document -> care -> secure base -> explore

  Without a first base, the circle is vicious: no one has enough shared context to educate the next participant without authority reappearing. With a protocol, the circle can become
  virtuous. The initial/free or "pure form" of Hoapa is the seed that breaks into that circle where no base yet exists. Initiality is therefore not only a least-commitment
  mathematical property. In this reading, it is the bootstrap of the care loop.

  This final section can be read either from the metaphysics or from the conjecture. From the metaphysics, openness is the best strategy for contextual transformation without
  collapse. From the conjecture, openness is the structural behavior the adjunction and its cascade are trying to make optimal. The two readings support each other, but neither
  proves the other.
