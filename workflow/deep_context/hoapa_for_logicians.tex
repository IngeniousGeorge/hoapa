\section*{Universe of contexts}

\[
  \UC = \text{universe of contexts},
  \qquad
  \UC_n = \{\Ctx,\Ag,\Sym\}.
\]

Here
\begin{align*}
  \Ctx & = \text{context } n, \\
  \Ag  & = \text{agents (unityped)}, \\
  \Sym & = \text{set of symbols}.
\end{align*}

The upper-right sketch gives the emergence sequence
\[
  \Ctx \xrightarrow{\text{emergence}} \Ag
       \xrightarrow{\text{emergence}} \Sym.
\]
The set of symbols is used for \emph{typed documentation}. Such
documentation is about the context and is produced by the agents; it gives
rise, by another emergence step, to the next universe of contexts:
\[
  \text{typed documentation}
  \xrightarrow{\text{emergence}} \UC_{n+1}.
\]

\section*{\textquotedblleft Theory of everywhere\textquotedblright}

The central evolution chain is
\[
  \UC_n \xrightarrow{\Ag} \Doc^{\mathrm{TT}}
        \xrightarrow{\Evol} \UC_{n+1}.
\]
It is annotated as \emph{not a series, but a network}.

The page then groups the following terms as a category, with the annotation
\emph{objects, morphisms}:

\section*{Editorial notes}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.1em]
  \item The handwritten $d\text{-}c$ in the natural example is legible as
        those characters, but its expansion is not given.
  \item $\Doc^{\mathrm{TT}}$ is the most likely reading of the middle object
        in the evolution chain; the superscript is handwritten as
        \(\mathrm{TT}\).
  \item The braces around $\{\Evol,\UC_n,\Hoapa\}$ and the label
        \emph{category} preserve the page grouping without inferring which
        listed terms are intended as objects and which as morphisms.
\end{itemize}
