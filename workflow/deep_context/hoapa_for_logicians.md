# Hoapa for logicians

Transcription of [the handwritten source](./hoapa_for_logicians.jpg). The layout
has been normalized for readability. Question marks and the final notes record
marks whose reading or intended meaning is uncertain.

## Universe of contexts

$$
UC = \text{universe of contexts}
$$

$$
UC_n = \{Ctx, Ag, Sym\}
$$

where:

- $Ctx$ = context $n$
- $Ag$ = agents (unityped)
- $Sym$ = set of symbols $\longrightarrow$ typed documentation

The upper-right sketch appears to say:

$$
Ctx \xrightarrow{\text{emergence}} Ag
\xrightarrow{\text{emergence}} Sym
$$

Typed documentation is **about** the context, **by** the agents, and made from
the set of symbols. From typed documentation there is another **emergence** into
$UC_{n+1}$.

## "Theory of everywhere"

$$
UC_n \xrightarrow{Ag} Doc^{TT}
\xrightarrow{Evol} UC_{n+1}
$$

This is **not a series, but a network**.

The page then groups the following as a category, with the annotation
"objects, morphisms":

$$
\underbrace{\{Evol, UC_n, Hoapa\}}_{\text{Category}}
\qquad (\text{objects, morphisms})
$$

## Condition

$$
Ctx \text{ endowed with perspectivalism / modalities}
$$

## Uncertain readings

- The handwritten $d\text{-}c$ in the natural example is legible as those
  characters, but its expansion is not given.
- $Doc^{TT}$ is the most likely reading of the middle object in the evolution
  chain; the superscript is handwritten as `TT`.
- The braces around $\{Evol, UC_n, Hoapa\}$ and the note "Category" preserve
  the page's grouping, without inferring which listed terms are intended as
  objects and which as morphisms.
