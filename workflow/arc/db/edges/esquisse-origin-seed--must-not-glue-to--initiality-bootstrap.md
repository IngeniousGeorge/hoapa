A fenced-off identification guarding the framework's temporality: the historical origin (the Grothendieck seed) must not be mistaken for the logical foundation (the produced fixed point). The seed is where the project came from; the bootstrap is the self-stabilising base it actually rests on, and these are different — conflating them would smuggle a "first instant" back in through history. Keeping them apart is exactly what lets the framework have an origin without having a ground.
