The bulk/boundary duality presupposes the exterior it is a duality *with*: the "reality-points" side of the Chu construction is the framework's constitutive exterior, which the "documentation-states" side charts through a lossy evaluation. Because that evaluation loses information, documentation never encloses reality — the duality is precisely what lets the exterior stay outside while still being charted, so it would be empty without [constitutive-exterior] to supply its points.
