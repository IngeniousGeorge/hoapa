The creativity-admissibility theorem presupposes the operator it disciplines: [creativity-transition-operator] is the move that carries one context-and-logic to another, and the theorem is the criterion under which such a move is allowed into the trace — that it resolve a typed obstruction, preserve required structure, and open verified constructions. Without the operator there would be nothing to admit; the theorem is the trace-side governance of the operator's leap.
