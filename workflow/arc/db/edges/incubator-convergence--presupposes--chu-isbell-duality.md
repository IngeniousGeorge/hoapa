The incubator's third description is as a Chu-points construction: the birthplace is read as the points side of the bulk/boundary duality, so bringing a new inquirer into being is presenting a new reality-point to be charted. This presupposes [chu-isbell-duality] as its semantic setting, and it is what ties the ethical reading (care's fixed point) and the foundational reading (the bootstrap) to a definite construction rather than leaving the convergence a metaphor.
