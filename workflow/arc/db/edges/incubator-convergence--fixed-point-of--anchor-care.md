The incubator is the fixed point of the care involution: the order-two "let differences stay independent" operation has, as its stable point, exactly the place where new inquirers and contexts are brought into being without being captured. This is why care is generative rather than merely protective — its fixed point is a birthplace — and it is one of three coincident descriptions of that point, the other two being a Chu-points construction and the initiality bootstrap.
