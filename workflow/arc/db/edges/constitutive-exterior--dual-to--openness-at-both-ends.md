The exterior and the openness constraint are the same refusal of self-collapse seen from the two ends: [openness-at-both-ends] names the inner end (no type of all types, a universe hierarchy), while [constitutive-exterior] is the outer end (no enclosed totality, a "reality" present only by absence). The perspective-flip between "there is no greatest container inside" and "there is always an unenclosed outside" is what makes this a `dual-to` rather than a dependency.
