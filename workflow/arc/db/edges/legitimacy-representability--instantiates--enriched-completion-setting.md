Legitimacy is the concrete reading, in practice terms, of a property internal to the enriched-completion setting: reflexive (Isbell) completeness, i.e. representability, i.e. "the foreign treated as one's own". So [legitimacy-representability] instantiates the general structure named by [enriched-completion-setting] — legitimacy is what that completeness looks like when the objects are inquirers and contributions rather than abstract profunctors.
