Logic and the metaphysics are two faces of one coin, related by the framework's
recurrent perspective-flip (its "dualising object"): the metaphysics describes how
worlds are made by typed transformations of context, while the logic describes, from
the inside, which transformations are valid. Reading "context produces
language-types" from the world's side gives the metaphysics; reading the very same
movement from the transformation's side gives the logic. Neither is prior, which is
why this is a `dual-to` and not a `presupposes`.
