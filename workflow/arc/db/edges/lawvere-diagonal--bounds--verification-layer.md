The diagonal result bounds the verification layer just as it bounds the community: the layer can check any particular claim, but it cannot be a complete internal account of its own correctness under classical negation, so it must stay stratified and revisable and can never become a closed sovereign. This is the guarantee that mechanised verification does not quietly become the new master — the impossibility of total self-verification is what keeps the layer an organ inside the community rather than an authority above it.
