The community's defining law, "type moves, not people", presupposes no-leak: unityped actors are indistinguishable except through their typed contributions precisely because identity cannot leak across the boundary. Without no-leak the single actor type would be a fiction — observers could recover who-did-what and re-stratify the community — so no-leak is the theorem that makes unityping real rather than aspirational, and it is what the anti-managerial design ultimately rests on at the boundary.
