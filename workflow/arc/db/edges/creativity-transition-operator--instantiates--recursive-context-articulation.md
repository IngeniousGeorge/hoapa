Creativity is the operator form of the recursion's own passage: where [recursive-context-articulation] describes, in the abstract, that a context yields a multiplicity that produces a new language-type, creativity is the actual move that does it — abductive retyping, saturation then liberation, the free leap answerable to what it forgets. The metaphysics names the shape of the transition; this operator is the transition being performed.
