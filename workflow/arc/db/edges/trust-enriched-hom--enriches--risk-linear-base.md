Trust is enrichment over the risk base: the hom-objects in which interactions are valued take their values in the linear/conserved structure that [risk-linear-base] provides, so trust is literally "measured in units of exposure". This is why trust cannot be freely duplicated or manufactured — it inherits the resource-sensitivity of the base it is enriched over, and spending it is spending something conserved.
