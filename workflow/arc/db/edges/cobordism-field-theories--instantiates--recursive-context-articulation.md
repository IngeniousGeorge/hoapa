A field theory is what the recursion becomes when its context-transformations are made local, compositional, and dualizable: boundaries are contexts, cobordisms are the passages between them, and gluing is the composition that carries one context into the next. This makes the physics not an analogy bolted on but a compositional instance of "context → multiplicity → language-type → new context", with the cobordism hypothesis pinning down when a single dualizable object already generates the whole theory.
