The protocol theorems are theorems *about and within* the verification layer: soundness, provenance, auditability, and the rest are statements the mechanised layer both enforces and lets one prove, so they presuppose it as their setting. Without the layer they would be aspirations about good conduct; with it they become checkable invariants of the trace — which is the difference between a code of ethics and a specification.
