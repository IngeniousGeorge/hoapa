The recursion presupposes an exterior it never encloses: "context → multiplicity → language-type → new context" can keep going only because there is always an outside it explores and forms perspectives on rather than internalises. Remove the constitutive exterior and the recursion would have to close into a final totality — precisely the move the framework forbids with its no-first-instant, no-final-container commitment.
