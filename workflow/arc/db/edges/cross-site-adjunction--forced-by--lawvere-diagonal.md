Why the central object is an *adjunction* (with a direction) and not a symmetric
equivalence is forced by the diagonal result: the two legs are asymmetric because one
site is rich in fixed points and the other poor in them. That asymmetry is the single
"if" the conjecture turns on — it is what makes Allow ⊣ Require an ordered pair rather
than a reversible correspondence, and it is inherited by every anchor that locates a
left-versus-right role.
