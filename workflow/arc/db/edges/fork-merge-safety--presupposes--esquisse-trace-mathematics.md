An individualised member edge: fork/merge safety presupposes the Esquisse trace-mathematics because recombining compatible branches is a descent problem — branches are local sections, their overlap is the compatibility datum, and a well-typed merge is a gluing. The pushouts, colimits, and sheaf-like descent that make such a merge preserve verified results are exactly what [esquisse-trace-mathematics] supplies, which is why this theorem is where the Grothendieck toolkit does concrete work inside the protocol family.
