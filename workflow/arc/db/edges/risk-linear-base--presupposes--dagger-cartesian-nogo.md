The conserved, non-copyable character of the risk base sits on the dagger-compact side of the no-go: risk can be the ambient linear quantity — exposure that cannot be freely duplicated or discarded — only because the no-go keeps that regime distinct from a copyable cartesian one. So the resource-sensitivity that makes risk, and hence trust and legitimacy, carry genuine cost presupposes the very incompatibility [dagger-cartesian-nogo] states; without it, "cost" would be forgeable by copying.
