The bootstrap is where the recursion closes on itself without a foundation: rather
than resting on a first context, the framework starts from a fixed point of its own
articulation — an initial / free model whose universal property produces the "secure
base" it seemed to need. This is the graph's honest near-root: not a node that
everything else presupposes, but a self-stabilising loop, which is exactly what the
ban on a first instant requires. It is the third temporal inversion the seed is built
to make visible (alongside care-upstream and openness-as-frame).
