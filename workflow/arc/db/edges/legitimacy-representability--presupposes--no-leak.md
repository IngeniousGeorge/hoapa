An individualised member edge completing legitimacy's two grounds: alongside the initiality bootstrap, legitimacy presupposes no-leak. Representability — being determined by one's total pattern of relations rather than by a granted privilege — can only serve as the criterion of legitimacy if identity does not leak around that pattern; otherwise status could re-enter through the back door. So no-leak is what stops "earned universality" from collapsing back into reputation-by-identity, and together with [initiality-bootstrap] it makes legitimacy produced rather than granted.
