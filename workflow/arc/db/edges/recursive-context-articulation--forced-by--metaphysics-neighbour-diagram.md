The proposition is not posited freely; it is forced as the shared shape — colimit,
"dépassement" — of the neighbour diagram. Each neighbour isolates one aspect
(Hegelian mediation, Deleuzian difference, Whiteheadian process, Peircean semiosis,
Lautmanian saturation/liberation), and the proposition is precisely what lets all of
them map in without collapsing into one another. This edge carries the graph's "why
it is convincing": the shape is derived from a standing diagram of established
positions, not chosen for taste.
