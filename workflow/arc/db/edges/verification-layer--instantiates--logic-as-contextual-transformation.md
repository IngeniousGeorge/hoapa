The verification layer is the discharge moment of borrow–work–give-back built as infrastructure: a claim borrows assumptions, work produces a proof object, and the checker discharges the dependency publicly, returning a reusable verified form. So the layer does not add a new kind of logic on top of the framework's; it is [logic-as-contextual-transformation] with its fourth moment — discharge/return — mechanised, which is precisely what lets verification be public and impersonal rather than a matter of someone's word.
