A region-targeted (mutualised) edge: trust, legitimacy, risk, and accountability only have content within a running community — they are the quantities earned, exposed, and traced in the interaction, not properties an isolated agent could carry. So every member of [praxis-nexus] presupposes [concurrent-community-of-inquirers], and the presupposition distributes across the region unless a member overrides it.
