An individualised member edge: legitimacy specifically presupposes the initiality bootstrap, because "earned universality" needs a produced-not-given secure base to be earned against. The free/initial universal property is what lets a contribution be determined by its total pattern of relations rather than by a granted privilege, so representability-as-legitimacy rests on [initiality-bootstrap] together with "no-leak". This is one of the two concrete grounds that keep legitimacy from becoming mere reputation.
