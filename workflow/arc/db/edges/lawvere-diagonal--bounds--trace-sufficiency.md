The diagonal result bounds the flagship: trace-sufficiency says every legitimate decision factors through the trace, but Lawvere's theorem forbids the trace from being a completed, self-total account under classical negation. So the flagship must be read as "sufficient, yet never closed" — the trace suffices for each decision without ever becoming an absolute that decides its own completeness. This bound is what stops trace-sufficiency from tipping over into a new sovereignty, keeping it a working criterion rather than a totalising claim.
