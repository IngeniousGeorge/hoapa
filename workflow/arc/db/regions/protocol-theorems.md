The family of theorems the typed verification layer is meant to yield — not statements about the world directly, but invariants of collective inquiry. Every member presupposes the [verification-layer] as its setting (a mutualised edge from the region), while individual members carry their own edges: the anti-privilege members to [no-leak], fork/merge safety to the Grothendieck trace-mathematics, responsibility to the accountability back-edge of the praxis nexus, creativity-admissibility to the creativity operator and the obstruction theorem. Its flagship is [trace-sufficiency]: every legitimate decision factors through the typed trace. Treat the region as semi-autonomous per the schema; derived in `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
