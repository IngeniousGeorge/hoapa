The mechanised organ that discharges the formalisable part of inquiry — proof assistants (Lean, Coq, Agda) checking types, proofs, dependencies, and consistency-relative-to-assumptions — so that verification is a public operation on trace-objects rather than a privilege held by any person. It replaces "trust me, I checked it" with "here is the proof object; anyone can run the checker", which is the concrete anti-managerial move: it removes one whole source of hierarchy without installing a new one, provided the layer itself stays editable by the community. Drawn from `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`, it is the discharge step of [logic-as-contextual-transformation] turned into infrastructure — and the very thing [lawvere-diagonal] forbids from ever becoming a complete internal account of its own correctness.
