The reading of exploration as a catalytic factorization: a territory together with an explorer is transformed into a book (a chart) together with the same explorer — the explorer is borrowed and returned unchanged, while the territory is what does the work of becoming charted. The "geographer" is the plain-English image (after Saint-Exupéry). Because the catalyst is conserved across the reaction, this is a clean instance of "borrow – work – give back", and its 0-truncation into a buildable artifact links it to the chart/book side of practice. It instantiates [logic-as-contextual-transformation]; see `workflow/arc/13_catalysts_and_care.md`.
