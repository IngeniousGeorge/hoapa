The reading of accountability as the dual (star) back-edge to trust: for every forward commitment there is a return edge along which responsibility can be traced, so accountability is the *-dual that makes the trust structure two-way. Whether this pairing is merely a duality (an involution) or a full adjunction (with unit and counit) is an open fork recorded in `workflow/arc/14_trust_and_legitimacy_as_enrichment.md`; the [accountability-dual-edge]--dual-to--[trust-enriched-hom] edge encodes the conservative reading. It is a member of the [praxis-nexus] region.
