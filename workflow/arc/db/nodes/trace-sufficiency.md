Trace-sufficiency, the flagship: every legitimate system decision factors through the typed trace, so the system never needs a privileged human authority outside the trace to justify acceptance, rejection, revision, merging, or repair. It is the formal heart of the project — "authority lives in the work, not the person" — and it presupposes [no-leak], without which the trace could not carry the whole of authority. It is held in explicit tension with [lawvere-diagonal], which forbids the trace from ever being a completed absolute: sufficient for every decision, yet never closed. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region.
