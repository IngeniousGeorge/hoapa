The historical seed of the whole project: three of Grothendieck's late writings mapped onto its three pillars — *Pursuing Stacks* onto the modal-HoTT side, *Esquisse d'un Programme* onto the concurrency side, and *Récoltes et Semailles* onto the motivation/praxis — recorded at the opening of the arc (arc file 01). It is deliberately given few logical edges: it is where the project *came from*, not what it *rests on*, and treating the origin as a foundation is exactly the seductive error the framework warns against (its start is a produced fixed point, not a given ground — see the `must-not-glue-to` edge to [initiality-bootstrap]). Its one structural role is self-referential: the founding of Hoapa is itself an instance of a context producing a new language.
