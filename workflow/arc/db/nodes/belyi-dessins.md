Belyi's theorem — a smooth projective curve is defined over a number field iff it admits a map to the projective line ramified only over {0, 1, ∞} — together with its combinatorial traces, the dessins d'enfants (bipartite graphs on surfaces). It is the paradigm "trace-compiler": a childlike finite mark carrying deep arithmetic (Galois) structure, which is what the community's trace layer aspires to. We fold in the allied theme that a small symmetry can unfold into an exceptional structure — the alternating group A₅ (icosahedral symmetry), through the binary icosahedral group and the McKay correspondence, reaching the E₈ lattice (Baez). It is the concrete instance behind [esquisse-trace-mathematics]; see `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
