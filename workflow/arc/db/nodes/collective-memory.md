Collective memory: if a claim was rejected, repaired, deprecated, or superseded, then any future reuse must carry that status unless a valid repair or translation is supplied — the system remembers its failures. It is one of the most practically consequential members, turning the trace into an institution that does not silently relitigate settled errors or resurrect refuted claims. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it rests directly on [provenance], since remembering a claim's fate requires its lineage to be intact.
