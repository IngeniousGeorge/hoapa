The explicit diagram whose colimit — its "dépassement", to use the arc's word — is
[recursive-context-articulation]: Hegel and Deleuze supply the seeding tension
(mediation-through-negation versus affirmative difference), joined by Whitehead
(process), Peirce (triadic semiosis), Simondon (individuation), Lautman and Zalamea
(saturation/liberation as mathematical praxis), and Lawvere (adjoint unity-of-opposites,
cohesion). It is a meta-module: the proposition is positioned not as a new doctrine
but as the shared shape into which each neighbour maps while keeping what matters.
Derived in `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`. A parallel diagram exists for
the inner notion of logic — Dewey, Girard, Lawvere, Martin-Löf, Abramsky,
Pym/O'Hearn, institution theory — and deserves its own node later.
