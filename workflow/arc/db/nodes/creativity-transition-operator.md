Creativity given a formal home as the operator that carries one context-and-logic to another, rather than as an inference inside a fixed one. Its micro-logic is Peircean abduction (proposing a new type for what had no place in the old grammar); its rhythm is Lautmanian saturation then liberation; its categorical image is the free / left-adjoint leap answerable to what it forgets. It is transformational in Boden's sense: it changes the space of possibilities, and so cannot be fully domesticated inside any one formalism — it becomes visible only after producing the form that makes it visible. It is the operator form of the passage in [recursive-context-articulation]; see `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
