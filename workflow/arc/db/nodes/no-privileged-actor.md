No-privileged-actor: system acceptance depends only on the typed trace and its proof obligations, not on the social identity or status of the inquirer — two unityped inquirers submitting equivalent moves with equivalent certificates are treated identically. This is one of the family's central anti-managerial results, and it folds in "role-erasure": any apparent authority reduces to a property of trace contributions rather than a primitive privilege. As a member of [protocol-theorems] it presupposes [no-leak], since identity must not leak for equal treatment to hold.
