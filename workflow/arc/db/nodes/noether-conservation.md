Noether's theorem read as the technical — not metaphorical — content of "borrow – work – give back": a continuous symmetry of the action yields a conserved current, so a transformation may rearrange a local region but must leave an invariant accounting through the boundary. A quantum anomaly is then a precise "failed giving-back": a classical symmetry that cannot be discharged consistently, which becomes a typed obstruction forcing a new context. This grounds the parallel between the framework and field theories at the level of conserved flow rather than analogy; it is a field-theoretic instance of [logic-as-contextual-transformation].
