The bridge from typed traces to physics: a field theory read as a machine that assigns data to contexts (boundaries) and to transformations between them (cobordisms), with gluing as composition. The cobordism hypothesis is the hinge — fully dualizable objects classify fully extended framed topological field theories — which formalises the framework's "flip of perspective" as the coherent duality (evaluation/coevaluation) an object must carry. Adding geometry, scale, and local observables (factorization algebras) climbs the ladder from topological to conformal to quantum field theory. It is a compositional, dualizable instance of [recursive-context-articulation]; see `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
