The reading of trust as the enriched hom-object between inquirers: not a scalar reputation but the structured "distance" that lives in the enriching base, so that trust is the very thing an interaction is valued in. It is what has to be earned and is carried by the trace rather than by status. It is a member of the [praxis-nexus] region and is enriched over the conserved base [risk-linear-base]. See `workflow/arc/14_trust_and_legitimacy_as_enrichment.md`.
