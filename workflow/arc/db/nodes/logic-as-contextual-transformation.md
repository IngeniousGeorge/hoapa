The framework's account of what logic is: not primarily inference over
propositions, but the disciplined act of borrowing from a context, performing work,
and discharging the dependency as a reusable form — the image is "borrow – work –
give back". Its sharpest proof-theoretic picture is Gentzen–Prawitz–Dummett
introduction/elimination with assumption discharge, but the same structure is
recovered from Tarskian model theory, Girard's linear logic, category-theoretic
internal logic, and Dewey's logic-as-inquiry (all surveyed in
`workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`). We treat it as the inner engine of
[recursive-context-articulation]: the metaphysics says worlds are made by typed
transformations of context; this node says which transformations are valid.
"Borrow – work – give back" is our plain-English image for the five-moment cycle
dependency → use → transformation → discharge → recontextualisation.
