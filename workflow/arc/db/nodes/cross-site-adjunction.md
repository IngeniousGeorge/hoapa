The central conjectured object: an adjoint pair between the modal-homotopy-type-theory
side [modal-cohesive-hott] and the concurrent-community side
[concurrent-community-of-inquirers], written in project notation as Allow ⊣ Require.
The left adjoint (Allow) unfolds modal perspectives into a running community; the
right adjoint (Require) reflects the community's operational separation back into
perspectival type theory. It is modelled on Lawvere's "unity and identity of
opposites" / adjoint-cylinder reading of dialectics and on cohesion's adjoint
modalities. An earlier three-slot version, with a middle "Mediate", is recorded in
the now-largely-stale `workflow/naming.md`; the current reading is the pair, with
the middle-slot question left open. This is the single object the whole conjecture
is about.
