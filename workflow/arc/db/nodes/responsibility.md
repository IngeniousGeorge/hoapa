Responsibility: every accepted intervention carries a typed responsibility path — authoring, dependencies, known risks, repair obligations, and affected contexts — so responsibility follows transformations rather than offices. It is the trace-level realisation of the accountability back-edge from the praxis nexus, and it matters most where interventions are ecological, technical, or political and someone must be answerable. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it is how "who is answerable" is derived from the work rather than assigned to a role.
