Normalization / redundancy: certain trace transformations can be simplified without changing their meaning or verified consequences — the trace analogue of proof normalization and cut-elimination. It is what lets duplicated arguments, unnecessary mediation, and bureaucratic clutter be compressed away, keeping the trace legible as it grows rather than accreting into an unreadable ledger. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it is the maintenance theorem that keeps the other guarantees usable at scale.
