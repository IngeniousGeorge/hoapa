Noninterference: metadata such as rank, prestige, institution, or identity label cannot affect a verification outcome unless explicitly typed as relevant evidence in the trace. It is the mechanically-checkable form of "social status cannot secretly influence acceptance", and it is essentially [no-leak] promoted from a property of the boundary to a property of the whole acceptance procedure. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it is stronger than fairness rhetoric precisely because it is an invariant one can run a check against.
