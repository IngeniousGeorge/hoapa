A standing constraint: the framework admits neither a greatest inner container (no
"type of all types", resolved by a Grothendieck-style / Martin-Löf universe hierarchy)
nor a completed outer totality (no topos of all toposes; the exterior it calls
"reality" is never enclosed). These are the same refusal of self-collapse seen from
two ends, and both are underwritten by [lawvere-diagonal]. The constraint is what
lets the framework keep its metaphysical promise of no first instant and no final
container. See `workflow/arc/11_reality.md`.
