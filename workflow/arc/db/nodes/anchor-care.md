A transverse structure, not one of the four adjoint parts: an order-two involution —
a dualising, self-inverse operation — that we read as Peirce's agape, the
"anti-managerial affect" that lets independent inquirers stay independent while
remaining open to common truth. It is a *condition on* the community rather than a
*product of* it, so in dependency order it sits upstream of the verification
machinery. "Care" is our plain-English image; the structural content is the
involution. This is one member of the [anchors] region.
