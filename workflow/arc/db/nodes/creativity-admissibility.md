Creativity admissibility: a new type, language, or context-extension is admissible when it resolves a typed obstruction, preserves the required prior structure, and opens verified new constructions — creative rule-change is allowed, but must leave a trace of why it was needed and what it preserves. It is the family's formal room for innovation without arbitrary rule-breaking, the place where the free leap stays answerable to what it forgets. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it presupposes both the [creativity-transition-operator] it disciplines and the [obstruction] its admissibility criterion refers to.
