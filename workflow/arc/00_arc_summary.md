## Graph edge palette

 1. Edge palette

  The 4 fields I gave (provides / presupposes / forced-by / fails-without) are really three of them are edges pointing outward: presupposes →, forced-by →, and the implicit provides
  is the reverse of someone else's presupposes. So the graph is already multi-typed. The design principle I'd commit to: the edge ontology should be a subset of the framework's own 
  relation-vocabulary — the doc's wiring is drawn from the theory it describes. That's itself a harmony proof, and very Lautmanian (the relations are the same "dialectical" relations
  the math uses).

  Candidate edge types, grouped:

  Dependency (directed, the backbone)
  - presupposes — A can't stand until B stands. (essential)
  - forced-by — A exists to resolve a tension in B; the necessity/derivation edge. (essential — carries the "convincing")
  - fails-without — reverse-lit: remove B, A breaks. Often the inverse of presupposes but not always (some are load-bearing without being presupposed — e.g. Care). (essential)

  Duality / harmony (symmetric or paired — these are the cycles)
  - adjoint-to — the co-arising pairs: Allow⊣Require, F⊣U, entropy⊣language. (essential — the harmony spine)
  - dual-to / flips-to — dualizing-object / perspective-flip: bulk↔boundary, "world learns to be inhabited," dagger. (essential)
  - enriches — A adds V-structure over B (trust as enriched hom; typed traces over raw traces). (keep)

  Motor (Lautman — process edges, the "temporality" that isn't a timeline)
  - saturates / liberates — A completes B; B releases a mobile form. This is your unexpected-temporality engine in edge form. (essential — very on-theme)
  - discharges-into — the borrow-work-return edge; carries a conserved accounting (Noether). (keep)

  Negative / limit (the constitutive exterior as edges)
  - bounds / obstructs — B says what A cannot do (Lawvere bounds verification; anomaly obstructs discharge). (essential — the "outside" is edges, not just a node)
  - must-not-glue-to — the fenced-off seductive-but-wrong identifications (bias_corrections: R4≠Allow⊣Require, buffer≠!, mediation≠descent). (keep — rare but crucial for a harmonious
  build; it marks the traps)

  Cross-context (Grothendieck / Abramsky)
  - translates-to / transports-along — explicit context-crossing, no smuggling. (keep)
  - glues-with / descends-to — local modules composing globally under compatibility, with descent-obstruction. (keep — this is how the tags cohere, see below)

  Realization (self-instantiation)
  - instantiates / realizes — A is a concrete resolution of abstract B (Realization.agda realizes audit-spec). (essential — this is what replaces "three scales")
  - fixed-point-of — reflexive loop; A is stabilized by an operation on itself (self-editable rules, initiality bootstrap). (keep — the honest "entry points")

## The pendulum diagram

 What the pendulum became

  Four positions + six invariant edges. Positions: Anchor = immanence (the point of suspension), Left pole = intelligibility / internal logic / law, Right pole = creativity / genetic
  logic / problem, Equilibrium = trace-practice / local-global reconstruction. The six repeatable edges are formalization (anchor→left), problematization (anchor→right), the poles' 
  polemic (left↔right), verification (left→equilibrium), stabilization (right→equilibrium), and recontextualization (equilibrium→anchor, which spawns the next pendulum).

  Instances: philosophy (Spinoza / Hegel / Deleuze / Peirce), mathematics (Riemann / Lawvere / Lautman / Grothendieck), science (Bacon-Galileo / Newton-Einstein / Darwin / Peirce),
  plus physics/biology/psychology sub-pendulums. Peirce is the hinge between the philosophy and math pendulums.

  Then it turns reflexive — three things I think matter most for us:
  1. The logic pendulum, where the equilibrium is our framework ("Trace Logic"), and it's declared orthogonal to the poles, not a midpoint: it asks "how does either side become
  publicly inhabitable?" → a logic of transformations/accountability.
  2. Three logic families — internal ("what follows?"), genetic ("what can emerge?"), trace ("what can be inherited?") — the third being, per ChatGPT, largely missing as an explicit
  philosophical object, i.e. the framework's own contribution.
  3. The culminating reframe: the equilibrium is more precisely a "logic of inheritance" — borrow → work → repay by leaving an inheritable trace; orphan = failed inheritance; agape =
  non-coercive inheritance. Flagged as possibly the original idea of the whole conversation, and it pulls Peirce toward the anchor of the entire construction (note: Peirce's
  position is mobile — equilibrium in philosophy, anchor in logic).

  There's also a Deleuze/Hegel refinement (from Logique du Sens and Science of Logic) inserting a middle layer: context → evental sense-surface | genesis of categories →
  type/proposition → trace → new context.

## Workilibrium logic

3.

b) "Logic of inheritance" — a beneficial sharpening of one axis, with two ways it can deteriorate

  Where it genuinely improves the concept. It names the third logic-family ("what can be inherited?") that really is under-theorized, and — crucially — it's not a slogan, because it
  grounds in nodes already in the graph: provenance, collective-memory, no-leak, conservative-self-editing are all inheritance guarantees, and orphan = failed inheritance is
  literally the Grothendieck Récoltes et Semailles wound. It also sharpens borrow-work-return from the vague "discharge the dependency" to the contentful "repay by leaving something
  the next context can inherit," and it re-anchors agape structurally as "the condition of non-coercive inheritance" (vs. appropriation/burial). That's a real unification.

  Two ways it deteriorates the concept if left as-is:

  1. It eclipses the lateral/ambient obligation. "Valid transformation under context" has two faces: don't damage the milieu you're embedded in now (no-leak, Noether boundary
  conservation, permaculture Earth-Care) and leave something for later. "Inheritance" foregrounds the diachronic and can quietly drop the synchronic. The save is available but must
  be made explicit: read inheritance as bidirectional stewardship — receive intact and pass on enriched (niche construction, permaculture soil), not merely "emit a trace for
  successors." If it collapses to trace-for-the-future, it loses the conservation face that Noether and no-leak carry, and that's a genuine loss.
  2. The name collides with type-theory/OOP "inheritance." In a project saturated with type theory, "logic of inheritance" is a false friend — readers will hear
  subclassing/subtyping. That's precisely the polysemy your naming discipline says to disambiguate. Options: keep the phrase but gloss it hard in-body as a double-quoted image, or
  reach for a term without the collision — "logic of transmission" or, most on-brand, lean on Peirce's own word, synechism/continuity (his whole corpus is "how continuity is
  preserved without suppressing novelty," which is this idea). My lean: name the node neutrally (e.g. logic-of-transmission or trace-logic) and keep "logic of inheritance" as the
  quoted image inside it.

  And one guardrail against tautology: ChatGPT admits it becomes "almost tautological." It's saved from that only by staying coupled to its failure modes — orphan, leak,
  appropriation, silent loss. The concept is contentful exactly to the degree it forbids those. So the node should define inheritance through what breaks it, not just assert the
  happy case.

  Verdict: net beneficial — it's the better name for the diachronic face of the logic the framework already has, and it pulls the Grothendieck-ethics and agape material into the
  logic proper. But scope it as the equilibrium of the logic pendulum specifically, not as a wholesale replacement for "valid transformation under context"; frame it as bidirectional
  stewardship so it keeps the conservation face; disambiguate the name from CS-inheritance; and define it through its failure modes. With those four moves it enriches the concept;
  without them it thins it.
