## Connes — noncommutative geometry & modular flow

**Who.** Alain Connes, with various collaborators (Rovelli on thermal time; Changeux on philosophy of mind in *Matière à pensée*, 1989).

**What they use.**
- *Noncommutative geometry.* A "space" is a *-algebra of observables rather than a set of points. Gelfand duality recovers ordinary topology when the algebra is commutative; when it isn't, the space has no jointly resolvable points. Core tool: spectral triples (A, H, D).
- *Modular flow* (Tomita–Takesaki). To any faithful state φ on a von Neumann algebra M, attach a canonical one-parameter automorphism group σ_t^φ : M → M — an intrinsic "time" generated by the (algebra, state) pair, without an external clock.

**What it models.**
- *Thermal time hypothesis* (Connes–Rovelli, physics): physical time is the modular flow of a thermal state on the observable algebra.
- *Consciousness, speculatively*: mind = a state on a noncommutative algebra of mental observables; the felt "now" = the modular flow that state induces. Mental observables don't commute (attention to one feature disturbs another), so the underlying space cannot be a manifold of states. Mostly interview-level rather than published technical work.

**Relation to our arc.** Loose. Shared high-level commitment: the algebra/topos of observables/perspectives is primitive, and structure flows from its internal logic. Its operator-algebraic setting differs from the project's earlier cohesive formulation; the M rebuild leaves the contextual setting open. No common mathematical construction is established. The convergence, if any, would be at the level of "perspectives are first-class," not at the level of constructions.

## Bennequin / Belfiore / Laforgue — topos theory for cognition (Huawei)

**Who.** Daniel Bennequin, Jean-Claude Belfiore, and collaborators (Huawei research line on topos-theoretic foundations for AI / cognition). Adjacent: Olivia Caramello's program on toposes as "bridges."

**What they use.**
- Topos theory: sheaves over sites of "perspectives," with adjoint structure organizing how local data glues into global.
- Information-theoretic and cohomological tools layered on the topos (information cohomology à la Baudot–Bennequin).
- Geometric morphisms between toposes used to model translation between cognitive / representational frames.

**What it models.**
- Cognitive architectures, deep learning systems, and information flow, formalized as sheaves on a site of contexts. The site is the structure of perspectives; the sheaf is the assignment of locally meaningful data; the topos provides the logic in which the assignment is reasoned about.

**Relation to our arc.** Direct family resemblance. Shared substrate: topos as the right setting for a logic of perspectives, with adjoint structure governing how perspectives relate. Their applied target (cognition / AI architectures) and our target (a contextual account of mathematical praxis) differ, and the specific cohesive triple ʃ ⊣ ♭ ⊣ ♯ is not (to my knowledge) central to their work — but the framing is the closest neighbour we have. Worth tracking: if their information cohomology lines up with our cover/recursion/network fragment, the math fragments may be the same object viewed from different applied directions.

## Social choice and discourse sheaves — constrained comparisons

**Who.** Arrow and the Kirman–Sondermann analysis of decisive coalitions;
Chichilnisky and Heal's topological social choice; Hansen and Ghrist's
discourse sheaves.

**What they use.** Arrow-style results constrain complete aggregation of
individual preference orderings under a specific package of domain,
unanimity/Pareto, independence, and non-dictatorship conditions.
Kirman–Sondermann express decisive coalitions through ultrafilters, which are
principal in the finite case. Under their topological domain hypotheses,
Chichilnisky–Heal characterize when continuous anonymous unanimous aggregation
exists for every finite panel size by contractibility of the preference space.
Hansen–Ghrist instead specify stalks and restriction maps on a communication
network and study opinion dynamics through the resulting discourse sheaf and
its Laplacian.

**Relation to our arc.** These are tests, not an inherited consensus theorem.
Hoapa permits failure and produces documented artifacts rather than assuming a
complete social ordering, so Arrow's hypotheses must be established before its
conclusion is imported. Contractibility would concern a specified space of
positions in Λ and a specified aggregation map, not panels or Λ as a whole.
Discourse sheaves show what must be supplied for a semantic sheaf model:
explicit local values and translations, beyond the actor-causal frame chassis.
The first useful comparison is the disagreement fixture in `future_work.md`:
two incompatible claims are preserved while their authors unanimously publish
an artifact recording the unresolved alternative. Human assent, documentary
fidelity, semantic compatibility, and gluing are separate predicates in that
case.

## Lautman — analytic/synthetic decomposition and recursive incarnation

**Who.** Albert Lautman, especially the two *Essais* on structure and existence and on the unity of the mathematical sciences. The reading used here also passes through Fernando Zalamea's account of saturation/liberation, Gilles Deleuze's transformation of dialectical Ideas into immanent problematics, and David Corfield's reconstruction of Lautmanian Ideas as immanent to mathematical practice. These readings must not be conflated. Lautman himself gives the Dialectic an ontological priority over the mathematics that realizes it; the immanent reading is a deliberate Deleuzian/Corfieldian transformation that is particularly suitable for this project.

**What they use.** Lautman distinguishes two ways of decomposing a mathematical being.

- A **proper, individual, or analytic decomposition** articulates what is peculiar to the entity under study. Prime factorization and the roots of a polynomial are model examples: the decomposition proceeds from a particular entity towards constituents that characterize it.
- A **dimensional or synthetic decomposition** follows a common plan supplied by the ambient domain. A vector is expressed relative to the dimension and a selected basis of its vector space; the whole determines a uniform mode in which each of its elements can be articulated. The decomposition expresses the "solidarity" of the elements through their shared domain.

The distinction is not an absolute separation. A supposedly proper decomposition also depends on an ambient domain: a polynomial may factor over one field and not another, and extension of the base may disclose constituents that were previously unavailable. Conversely, dimensional decomposition does not erase individuality; it gives individual entities coordinates relative to a shared structure. The important contrast is therefore between **individuation by a being's effective constituents** and **articulation according to a structure supplied by the whole**, with each capable of conditioning the other.

Lautman's broader dialectic gives this contrast a dynamic setting. Dialectical Ideas are problematic relations — local/global, whole/part, finite/infinite, continuous/discontinuous, intrinsic/extrinsic, structure/existence — that effective mathematical theories partially realize. The movement is double. From the side of genesis, an effort to understand a problem is extended in the construction of more concrete notions and theories. From the side of mathematical philosophy, one reads regressively from an effective theory to the problematic relations it incarnates. The priority of the problem is not chronological and is not an axiomatic deduction of mathematics from metaphysics; it is the priority of a question relative to a response, even when the question only becomes explicit after a response has been found.

**What it models.** The two decompositions model how individual differentiation and global organization arise together. They are useful when a homogeneous or uniformly presented field produces heterogeneous individual structure, when a particular object can only be analyzed relative to a surrounding domain, or when changing a basis, field, cover, context, or presentation changes the decomposition without necessarily changing the being presented. Saturation and liberation add a practical rhythm: incomplete structures are enriched, completed, or globalized; a more mobile invariant, notion, or interface is then liberated from the enriched construction and becomes usable in another context.

**Decomposition and genesis are distinct questions.** In
[the miscellaneous notes](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#lautmans-decompositions), the
passage beginning "Lautman's decompositions" separates how a being is
articulated from what that articulation makes possible. Proper/dimensional
decomposition addresses the first question; structure/existence addresses the
second. A decomposition may describe an existing being, while a schema of
genesis exhibits how the structure of one domain supports the existence of
further beings. For the mirror, ask when a disciplined separation enables a new
construction rather than merely another description. Not every decomposition is
generative.

**Mixtes as mediators.** Lautman's intermediate structures can enable passage
between different kinds of beings without identifying them. This gives a
source question for adapters and correspondence candidates: what must an
intermediate structure retain from each setting to make the passage possible?
It does not prescribe another category or identify R4 with a Lautmanian mixte.
See [Petitot, §§2.3.4–2.3.5](https://jeanpetitot.com/ArticlesPDF/Petitot_Lautman.pdf)
for these themes and their examples.

**Making a problematic relation mathematically explicit.** The notes' passage
[beginning "Grothendieck may therefore reverse Lautman's division of labour"](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#making-a-problematic-relation-mathematically-explicit)
suggests the following investigations, without establishing the historical
reversal claimed in its title:

- local/global becomes a question about restriction, compatibility, and descent;
- essence/existence can become representability of a specified functor;
- many realizations can lead to a moduli problem, retaining automorphisms through groupoids or stacks where needed;
- particular/universal can be articulated through a universal construction;
- continuous/discrete can be investigated through suitable cohesion structures.

These are examples, not an exhaustive translation of philosophy into
mathematics. Representability and moduli give question shapes for the
comparison structure and contextual variability; neither supplies Hoapa's
adequacy or P. Making a problematic mathematically explicit need not exhaust
its philosophical significance.

**Relation to our arc — the stated project interpretation.** What is being
decomposed is **understanding of the world**, existing in two distinct realms:
embodied understanding distributed among a community of thinkers, and
documented understanding articulated through concepts and their relations.
Hoapa organizes the former **predominantly synthetically**, through a common
unityped mode of participation, and the latter **predominantly analytically**,
through differentiated conceptual structures. This is an intended philosophical
and architectural reading, not a proved decomposition theorem.

The vector image expresses shared capacity with situated contributions; the
prime-factorization image expresses articulation into constituents specific
to what is understood. Neither asserts identical minds, a basis of people,
canonical conceptual primes, or unique factorization. Unityping supplies a
common formal interface, not equality of participants or of their abilities.

**Research genealogy.** In the notes beginning
["My contribution to epistemology"](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#research-genealogy),
the instigator reports that pursuing equal-standing participation alongside
differentiated documentation led to making the reality/representation boundary
explicit and investigating further documentary separations. This records a path
of discovery, not a derivation that the first architectural commitment
logically entails all the others.

These are dominant orientations, not exclusive assignments. Documentation
aspires to become synthetic by furnishing languages and structures for later
understanding. The political pressure in the opposite direction projects
documentary classifications back onto participants as fixed specialties and
hierarchies. Hoapa seeks differentiated knowledge without making its producers
into corresponding formal kinds. The following crossing elaborates, rather
than cancels, this orientation:

| Layer | Analytic / proper moment | Synthetic / dimensional moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational** | A particular execution is articulated into its actual events, choices, causal dependencies, branches, and unresolved obligations. | Executions take place through a common actor interface, protocol, interaction architecture, and causal-frame discipline. |
| **Contextual / documentary** | A particular artifact is articulated through its specific type, evidence, provenance, constructors, and dependencies. | Every artifact receives meaning from an ambient type context, universe, site, cover, reindexing structure, and descent discipline. |

The rejection of `Op = analytic; Ct = synthetic` as an exhaustive layer
assignment does not reject this reading of understanding in two realms.
Unityped actors retain histories, states, perspectives, and internal structure.
Documentation selects and types what may be retained; neither realm preserves
every distinction. Both decompositions also occur on M and K: neither category
is simply "the synthetic side" or "the analytic side."

**Architectural counterexample.** Fix otherwise identical eligibility and
protocol conditions, but let a participant's documentary output determine
their right to occupy a seat, speak, or exercise formal authority. Such a
system fails Hoapa's intended interface-grounded equality of standing. This is
the political concern as a testable architectural contrast: evaluating work
must not silently become ranking its producers' standing. It is not a theorem
that the rival has no synthetic organization, or that every departure from
unityping flips the decomposition. Conversely, the test must allow different
contributions to receive different assessments without changing their
producers' formal standing. Tier A alone does not establish this full property.

### Arbitrary occupants and represented work

Participants embody and contribute to the understanding being articulated;
they are not themselves the beings to be exhaustively decomposed. Lautman's
examples use "any" and "arbitrary" in more than one role: any entity of a
domain is articulated under the common plan, while an
auxiliary basic system or a particular solution may also be chosen from an
admissible class. The choice is not unconstrained and need not be irrelevant to
every result; its force is that no intrinsic privilege of one occupant is a
precondition of the construction.

This is closer to Hoapa's participant rule. Any eligible person may occupy a
seat and receives the same protocol-visible standing, while different people
and panels may still make different contributions. Participants help produce,
select, test, and revise articulations of shared work. They are neither a basis
of the population nor the completed product of a decomposition. Representation
therefore belongs primarily to runs, contributions, artifacts, concepts, and
their contextual relations. The trace may represent specified participation
without claiming to exhaust the person.

The catalyst and three-marked-sphere comparisons remain limited aids. The
catalyst image names participation without incorporation of a person into the
documentary product; it does not say that the person is unchanged by the work.
Three arbitrary distinct marks illustrate that occupants may be movable while
their number, distinctness, and relations are essential; it does not establish
that changing a panel preserves an achieved conversation. Neither comparison
introduces participant kinds, spanning, or a change-of-basis theorem.

### Base-relative articulation

A proper decomposition is not absolutely context-free: factorization can
change after extension of the coefficient field. The project should therefore
state causal and content properties relative to their realized run, context,
or documentation version. One first candidate is `SaturatedIn(R, c)`, saying
that every globally witnessed causal relation between events of conversation
`c` is already generated internally to `c` in run `R`. The banked
`ConversationOrderComparison` gives the pointwise saturation predicate and
both verdicts; making the base parameter and append-only extension law explicit
is future work.

The associated theorem-shaper is persistence of an unsaturation witness under
an extension that retains old events and edges. It would say that later
documentation cannot rewrite away a dependency already exposed in an earlier
base. This is a Lautman-shaped relative statement, not an identification of
causal saturation with completeness, encapsulation, or Simondonian
metastability.

### A cover as a higher basis

A cover of causal interaction contexts suggests a higher analogue of Lautman's basis. Restricting an object to the members of a cover yields local views analogous to coordinates. Overlaps and higher overlaps supply compatibility data absent from an ordinary vector-space basis. A satisfactory reconstruction should depend only on the underlying contextual object, up to the relevant equivalence, rather than on an arbitrary presentation by a selected cover.

The functor-of-points refinement makes the required bridge more exact. For a
chosen category `A` of locality objects and an interpretation `i : A → C`, the
nerve records the probes `C(i(−), X)`. The functor `i` is dense when the
canonical realization of all such probes recovers `X`. In Hoapa,
conversation frames are candidates for the locality objects; participants are
occupants and incidences inside them. Random selection gives no density
theorem, and neither one panel nor an unstructured succession of draws
represents the eligible population. Any reconstruction claim concerns a
specified documented context and must name its probe functor and hypotheses.

This analogy shapes questions but is not yet a theorem. Basis coordinates are unique once a basis is fixed; sheaf data require overlap compatibility, and invariance under refinement or replacement of a cover needs precise topology, cofinality, and descent hypotheses. One must also distinguish two variances that the phrase "free gluing" can blur: a context may be presented by a colimit-like Čech construction under suitable hypotheses, while compatible data over it are reconstructed by a limit/descent condition. The existing finite and point-generated results establish scoped descent and sheafification facts; they do not yet establish general Čech-colimit reconstruction, cover-refinement invariance, or basis independence.

Accordingly, sheaf descent alone does not prove "no privileged point of view." That interpretation requires further properties: equivariance under legitimate relabelings, independence from a chosen basepoint or chart, stability under cover refinement, and conservative reindexing between perspectives. Lautman's dimensional decomposition supplies a reason to look for this theorem family, not a proof of it.

### Transversal work, forgetting, and renewal

The project's `borrow → work → give back` logic runs transversally through the Lautmanian movement and recurs within its stages. It should not be assigned once as `metaphysics = borrow`, `mathematics = work`, and `physical/social reality = repayment`. A mathematical theory borrows a problematic field, works through effective constructions, and returns a new articulated problem-space; a communication process borrows a contextual affordance, performs situated work, and returns an accountable artifact plus a changed context; metatheoretic or reflective work can then take either construction as its own object. Work and preservation of future viability are primary. Replay and inheritance are possible consequences of a successful return, not definitions of the work itself. This cross-links the cartography nodes `logic-as-contextual-transformation` and `workilibrium-logic`.

The pre-philosophical image of infinity discussed in the same arc can be retained here in a limited role. A quest for further dimensions resembles saturation: adding enough relations, distinctions, modalities, or coordinates to articulate a problem. Purpose-relative forgetting resembles liberation: abstracting from a presentation, quotienting irrelevant detail, or extracting an invariant that can travel. Renewal occurs when that liberated form becomes a new context. The forgetting must state what it preserves, what it hides, and for which future operations it is adequate. Universe stratification and typing can control paradoxical self-totalization; they do not by themselves transform an endless hierarchy into a philosophically regenerative infinity.

Hilbert's theory/metatheory distinction is a nearby but separate second-order image. A formal theory can be retyped as an object for metamathematical study, and consistency or completeness information can then govern how the theory is understood or extended. This resembles an operational process becoming documentary material for a higher inquiry, but a Hilbertian metatheorem is not thereby a Lautmanian dialectical Idea, and metatheory is not assumption discharge performed "one floor up."

### Status and genuine extension

**Grounded in the current project:** the architectural separation between a uniform actor interface and typed artifacts; the actor-causal site and strict `FrameHom` discipline; finite descent; the supported point-generated reflector `a ⊣ ι`; the point-generated LNL bridge `Lpoint ⊣ Rpoint`; and the first generic raw-run-to-global-frame realization.

**Candidate readings and theorem-shapers:** the 2×2 Lautmanian interpretation; cover-as-higher-basis; refinement and presentation invariance; purpose-relative trace adequacy and replay; `Doc`/`Aff`; and recurrence of one abstract work pattern across mathematical, physical, ecological, and communicational domains. Recurrence across examples is abductive evidence, not a demonstrated scale-invariance theorem.

**Rejected identifications:** `Op = analytic`; `Ct = synthetic`; unityped = untyped or dimensionless; typed documentation = total memory; descent = no privileged viewpoint; metatheory = dialectic; `Doc ⊣ Aff = Allow ⊣ Require`; the checked point-generated bridge = the conjectural cross-site adjunction; and typing alone = a resolution of bad infinity.

Hoapa's genuine proposed extension is to make Lautman's already-double movement institutional, computational, recursive, public, and potentially self-modifying. Lautman already treats mathematics as a model for physical, social, and human modes of incarnation, so it would be inaccurate to describe his movement as merely diagnostic or one-shot. Hoapa instead attempts to give communicational incarnation an executable protocol and a typed trace boundary through which successes, failures, exclusions, and obstructions can feed back into subsequent mathematical and metaphysical articulation.

## Simondon — individuation and transduction

**Who.** Gilbert Simondon, especially his account of individuation as primary relative to already constituted individuals. The relevant concepts are the preindividual field, metastability, transduction, and the joint individuation of an individual and its milieu.

**What they use.** A metastable field contains unresolved potentials rather than a fully determined object. Individuation is the operation through which a local incompatibility is progressively resolved, structuring both an individual and the milieu in which further transformations remain possible. A successful individuation does not exhaust the preindividual reserve. Transduction names the stepwise propagation by which each local resolution supplies conditions for the next.

**What it models.** The view is useful wherever a context is not merely an external container and an individual is not a primitive atom: a particular form precipitates from a structured field, modifies that field, and leaves it capable of further individuation. It distinguishes stabilization from terminal closure and a developmental sequence from repeated copies of one finished object.

**Information and uptake.** The notes beginning
["Simondonian information should not be placed simply on the analytic/content pole"](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#simondonian-information-and-uptake)
sharpen a point in
[Simondon's account](https://parrhesiajournal.org/parrhesia07/parrhesia07_simondon1.pdf):
information concerns a problematic and the receiving system's transformation,
not merely a transmitted signal. For Hoapa, distinguish an accessible artifact
from its becoming informative in a particular encounter. The same artifact may
confirm an existing understanding, resolve an uncertainty, or reorganize a
documentary problematic. These are proposed applications of the source, not
quantities measured by Shannon entropy or a license to model participants'
interior lives in the kernel. See the uptake investigation in
[future_work.md](future_work.md#information-regeneration-and-succession).

**Relation to our arc.** Arc/16 uses Simondon to interpret an operational episode `w : L M → K₀` as a particular individuation within a contextual field, and its transpose `w♯ : M → R K₀` as that episode's contextual presentation. Arc/17 sharpens the placement: conversations and achieved artifacts individuate; participants arrive as already declared persons rather than being individuated by the protocol; and receptivity or unresolved potential belongs to the milieu and its available continuations rather than to an identity-refining participant property. A successful closure may still leave the context charged with unresolved dependencies or new possible work.

The universal / particular split aligns suggestively with Lautman's synthetic / analytic decomposition. This is an interpretation, not evidence: transposition is automatic for any adjunction, no class of admissible individuations has been defined, and persistent transduction would require the non-collapse obligation in `targets.md` §T3 plus the changing-context semantics investigated in `future_work.md`. Simondon shapes what counts as developmental success—renewal that preserves earlier achievements without exhausting the field—but supplies no theorem about `Allow ⊣ Require`. His account of good form already includes preservation and renewed capacity, so that criterion should not be attributed to the project's Care anchor alone. The terminology remains provisional pending a primary-text pass: in particular, the project's causal "saturation" must not be renamed metastability merely because the words suggest one another.

## Grothendieck's Esquisse — dessins, branch data, and the moduli tower

**Who.** Alexander Grothendieck's *Esquisse d'un Programme* and the mathematics around dessins d'enfants, Belyi maps, Teichmüller towers, the absolute Galois action, and the Grothendieck–Teichmüller group.

**What they use.** A dessin is a finite bipartite ribbon graph encoding a finite cover of the projective line ramified over at most three values, normalized to `{0, 1, ∞}` because the three-pointed genus-zero base is rigid. Monodromy records permutations around the punctures. The absolute Galois group acts faithfully on dessins and maps as `G_ℚ ↪ GT`; whether this map is surjective is open. The recursive large object is the tower of moduli / Teichmüller structures, not any single dessin.

**What it models.** A finite combinatorial presentation can retain global topological and arithmetic information, while a tower organizes how such presentations vary, glue, and transform across levels. It is therefore a strong source of tests for presentation invariance, controlled forgetting, monodromy preservation, and nontrivial comparison—but not a ready-made model of Hoapa's monad iteration.

**Relation to our arc.** Dessins occupy a dual but compatible position: they are far-end K-side moduli / cover objects and possible K-facing elements of a future cross-site mediator. The candidate technical form is the independently constructed, space-valued shape-witness profunctor recorded in `future_work.md`, whose later two-sided representability would recover T1 rather than constitute separate evidence for it. The principal mismatch is still open: the modal route currently offers a bare `S²`, while a dessin needs a three-marked base plus branch and cover data. The Esquisse material therefore supplies a gated test and a theorem-shaper, not an anchor or a constructed bridge.

### Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch as a Lautman–Simondon crossing

**A smaller worked example: local prescriptions and global realization.**
The miscellaneous notes' passage
["Riemann–Roch then makes the two decompositions meet"](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#riemann-roch-and-the-two-decompositions)
motivates this crossing. The following comparison is Codex's proposed
application, not an example copied from those notes.

Over the complex numbers, let L(P) be the space of meromorphic functions on a
smooth projective connected curve with at most a simple pole at P and no other
poles. On the projective line, dim L(P) = 2: a nonconstant function with
exactly that pole exists (z at infinity is an example). On a genus-one curve,
dim L(P) = 1: only constants remain, so a single simple pole is impossible.
These are consequences of
[Riemann–Roch](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B5B).
Locally one can prescribe a nonzero simple principal part in either case; its
global realizability differs.

This is an example for the reciprocal separation/integration mirror.
Separating local prescriptions from their ambient setting makes the integration
question precise. Conversely, the global framework reveals a distinction
between locally similar possibilities. More generally, the principal-parts
exact sequence records the obstruction to extending such data globally in
H¹(O). Neither local data nor global geometry is assigned exclusively to K or
M. This is an established mathematical example for the interpretation, not a
Hoapa theorem or a selected Agda build.

The following GRR comparison has a different role: transport of corrected
invariants, rather than this local-to-global existence question.

The Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch theorem is useful here as a historical and
mathematical theorem-shaper: a construction over a space is transported to its
base while retaining a corrected invariant, and the corrected comparison makes
individual data and ambient organization inseparable. In the longer movement
of arithmetic geometry, constituents disclosed by analysis can become
coordinates of a new synthetic domain: primes become points of `Spec(ℤ)`,
arithmetic becomes geometry over that base, fibers recover arithmetic at its
places, and Galois symmetries can then act on geometric or combinatorial
objects. These are related transformations, not one automatic step.

For Hoapa the corresponding candidate movement is that a conversation's
achieved output becomes an admissible coordinate or input of a later context.
The precise criterion is preservation with renewal: specified earlier
artifacts and judgments remain recoverable, while a genuinely unavailable
continuation becomes admissible. Tier B's relative change of actor base is a
small existing instance of the preservation shape; contextual renewal is the
larger open instance. The analogy supplies no Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch theorem
for Hoapa and no Galois action. Historical reuse of a theorem by later
mathematics is also not the project's technical recorder-fidelity or
purpose-relative `Replay`.

This crossing helps locate arithmetic without making it the coefficient of a
Chu pairing. Arithmetic is positive structure on a base and its categories,
not the constitutive exterior. A structured level-0 base may emerge from
level-−1 outputs even though eligible participants have no privileged
coordinate. The subsequent symmetry stage still needs an actual group, action,
and invariant—ultimately gated by the unbuilt `MO_1` and its arithmetic
geometry.

## A5 / icosahedral symmetry — detection heuristic, not anchor

**Who.** The classical icosahedral symmetry package: the alternating group A5, the rotation group of the icosahedron/dodecahedron, and its appearances in dessins, Klein's icosahedral work, McKay-flavoured exceptional structures, and finite symmetry models.

**What they use.**
- A5 as the smallest non-abelian simple group.
- Icosahedral incidence data: 12 vertices, 20 faces, 30 edges, 60 rotations.
- Stabilizer/orbit patterns such as A5 acting transitively on 12 vertices.
- Dessins/Belyi maps with icosahedral monodromy.

**What it models.** A compact, finite, highly constrained symmetry pattern. For this project it is not currently the concurrency anchor; the actor-causal + session-values hybrid and `O_1` interaction algebra are the active commitments. But A5/icosahedral structure may still reappear as a downstream finite witness of the larger cyclic/modular/dessin-facing machinery.

**Relation to our arc.** The regular degree-60 A5 dessin is a gated, finite test object. On the K side it lies beyond `MO_1`; as a comparison witness it also waits for the M side to acquire three-point branch / orbifold data. The primary test is whether both sides preserve compatible branch cycles and monodromy; the appearance of A5 comes second. One dessin can falsify a proposed mediator component or compatibility law, but cannot construct the adjunction, verify its naturality or triangle identities, or model its iteration.

The caution is equally important: numerical resonances alone are not evidence. For example, a `5 + 8 = 13` decomposition of constructors may smell icosahedral or golden-ratio-adjacent, but it is not A5 structure unless it survives repackaging and is explained by an actual symmetry, action, orbit, representation, or dessin. Treat these resonances as places to look, not conclusions.

Static A5 symmetry also says nothing by itself about idempotence: finiteness is a property of the test object or its automorphism group, whereas collapse is a property of the induced monad and its structure maps. The dessin supplies one calculable stage, not the transition maps of T_recursion. Genuine independently arising A5 structure remains worth noticing, but it is a test signal rather than support for the conjecture.

## Universal-theory precedents — the cybernetics-era pattern

**Common pattern, and relation to our arc.** Each of the entries below was framed as a universal theory with applications across engineering, biology, psychology, sociology, and politics. In every case, a real piece of mathematics (control theory; differential equations on networks; cellular automata) was over-extended into a universal framework whose interdisciplinary claims outran the formal content. Each collapsed in part because the unifying claim turned out to be slogan-level rather than theorem-level. Our project shares the *ambition* — a framework with natural readings across disciplines — but is structurally trying to avoid the failure mode by keeping the mathematics first and the interdisciplinary readings second, rather than the other way around. The cohesive ∞-topos / cyclic operad / factorization algebra material has its own categorical integrity; the cross-disciplinary readings are downstream observations, not load-bearing claims. The discipline of "math earns the interpretation, the interpretation does not float free" is the deliberate counter-move to this pattern.

The list:

- *Wiener's cybernetics* — control theory and feedback as a universal frame for engineering, biology, society.
- *Ashby's cybernetics* — variety, requisite variety, *Design for a Brain* — a homeostatic/adaptive-system formalism extended to cognition and ethics.
- *Bateson's cybernetics* — patterns connecting biology, learning, ecology, mental disorder, family therapy.
- *Pask's cybernetics* — conversation as a universal feedback structure (the praxis-including aspect of Pask's work is treated separately below).
- *Bertalanffy's General Systems Theory* — open systems and equifinality as a universal interdisciplinary frame.
- *Wolfram's A New Kind of Science* — cellular automata as a universal computational basis for natural science.

## Self-referential / praxis-including precedents

**Common thread, and relation to our arc.** Frameworks that include the *praxis* of their own use as part of their subject matter — the community that uses the framework is also (in some sense) what the framework is about. Closer to our project than the cybernetics-era precedents above, because they share the structural commitment to fold praxis into theory; weaker than our project at the level of mathematical depth, except for HoTT/UF which is comparable. Ordered roughly historically. Each entry names what is shared and what is missing relative to our combination.

**Peirce's community of inquiry.** The closest *philosophical* precedent. Peirce held that truth is what the community of inquiry would converge to in the long run, and that scientific practice is not a method *applied to* a community but a community *constitutive of* knowledge. The self-testing structure is there: the community's eventual convergence is the truth, so the community's work is *both* what produces truth and what tests whether it has been produced. What is missing: any formal mathematics underwriting the claim, any protocol the community could actually follow, any account of what counts as "convergence." Peirce gave the slogan; the technical content was deferred.

**Pask's conversation theory.** Gordon Pask (1928–1996) explicitly tried to build a theory whose subject matter was conversation, and which could be deployed in actual conversational systems (the CASTE / THOUGHTSTICKER tutorial environments). The theory tested itself: a learner using a Pask-based system either learned or did not, and the rate at which they did was held to be the theory's vindication. This is the closest historical analogue to our "if the work is fruitful, the protocol is validated" structure. What is missing: Pask had no access to modern categorical / homotopical machinery, so the mathematical content was thin (a calculus of agreements, a graph-theoretic model of concepts). The theory's claims about deep structure (the L-language, P-individuals) did not have a topos-theoretic underwriting. The closest there is, and the project should probably claim Pask as a direct predecessor more explicitly than it currently does.

**Maturana and Varela's autopoiesis.** A theory of self-producing systems intended to apply to cells, organisms, cognition, and (in Maturana's later "Biology of Cognition") social systems. Self-reference is constitutive: the theory describes systems that produce themselves, and the theorists claimed their work was an instance of what the theory described. What is missing: the formalisation never matured. Autopoiesis as a slogan survives; autopoiesis as a mathematical structure was never written down at the depth that, say, a topos-theoretic framing would require. And the praxis-loop was hand-wavy — there was no protocol attached.

**Luhmann's social systems theory.** Niklas Luhmann built a theory of society as communication, mathematically grounded in Spencer-Brown's *Laws of Form*. He explicitly claimed his theory described its own production as a social system — the books were communications about the theory of communication. Strikingly self-referential. What is missing: again, no protocol — Luhmann did not give sociologists tools, he gave them a descriptive frame. And the mathematical underwriting (Spencer-Brown) is much thinner than what the project would need.

**Univalent Foundations / HoTT community.** The closest *modern* precedent. The HoTT book was written collectively by a community using the foundations it described; the formal libraries (Coq, Agda, Lean) are the praxis arm; the community tests whether HoTT is a useful foundation by actually using it to formalise mathematics. The self-test loop is genuine: if HoTT is the right foundation, mathematicians will be able to formalise more and more of their work in it; the rate of that formalisation is, in some sense, the test. What is missing relative to our project: HoTT is a *foundation offered to a community*, not a *theory of the community using foundations*. The community is the user of HoTT, not the subject matter of HoTT. The two things are not quite the same — and the difference is what the "include praxis into the theory" formulation is pointing at.

**Active inference / free-energy principle (Karl Friston).** This is structurally interesting because it includes the agent–environment loop and treats "the agent's model is tested by acting on the environment" as constitutive of cognition. Friston's framework is a unified mathematical theory (Bayesian, variational) that claims to describe perception, action, biology, and (by extension) social systems. The self-evidencing loop is built in. What is missing: the framework is foundationally *single-agent*. Social applications are by analogy, not by formal extension to community-of-inquiry structure. And the praxis arm is not part of the theory — the framework describes agents from outside, it is not used by agents.

**Bourbaki.** A community-of-inquiry that produced a praxis (the *Éléments de mathématique*), with the meta-claim that "mathematics is what Bourbaki writes." Self-referential in the meta-claim. What is missing: no theory of the community itself; the praxis was *production of mathematics*, not *test of a theory about communities*.

### What none of them have

Scanning the list, no precedent has the specific combination our project is aiming at:

1. **Modern mathematical foundations** (cohesive ∞-topos, modular operads, factorization algebras) — only HoTT/UF and Friston have technical material at this altitude;
2. **A formal theory of the community-of-inquiry itself** as part of the framework's subject matter — Pask, Luhmann, Maturana–Varela aim at this, but at thinner formal depths;
3. **A protocol the community can actually run** — Pask had this in CASTE, HoTT/UF has it in the libraries; the others do not;
4. **The protocol's *use* validating the framework** — Pask, Friston, and (less explicitly) HoTT/UF have this; Peirce articulated it; the others do not;
5. **A recursive co-evolution structure** linking (1)–(4) into a single mathematical object — *no precedent has this*. The iterated cross-site adjunction with sequential colimit is, as far as we can tell, genuinely without forerunners that combine it with (1)–(4).

The closest individual matches are:

- **Pask** (has 2, 3, 4 at low formal depth)
- **HoTT/UF community** (has 1, 3, weak 4, no 2)
- **Peirce** (has 4 philosophically, lacks 1–3 formally)
- **Friston** (has 1, 4, weak 5, no 2 as community-of-inquiry)
- **Luhmann** (has 2 at low formal depth, weak 5)

The *combination* is, as far as we can see, novel. Not because the question is unprecedented — Peirce was asking it 130 years ago — but because the technical material to address it at depth is recent.

### Why now

The combination may not be merely *novel* but *newly available*. The technical conditions for assembling (1)–(5) were not in place until quite recently:

- Cohesive ∞-toposes are a 21st-century object (Lawvere's hints in the 1990s, Schreiber's development in the 2010s, Shulman's formalisation work ongoing).
- Modular operads as a tool for community-of-conversations structures presuppose Getzler–Kapranov 1998 and the categorical operad infrastructure since.
- Factorization algebras as the categorical framework for QFT are Costello–Gwilliam 2017.
- Linear logic / session types as a foundation for typed-message protocols is Caires–Pfenning 2010.
- The HoTT book is 2013.
- The existence of LLMs as plausible unityped participants in a protocol is a development of the last three or four years.

The earlier attempts (Peirce, cybernetics, Pask, Maturana–Varela, Luhmann) were trying to do this with the tools available to them, and the tools were insufficient. The mathematical content thinned out exactly where it most needed to be dense. The current moment is the first in which the technical machinery exists *and* there is a class of agents (LLMs) for which the question of community-of-equal-participants is operationally pressing.

That is also a defensive point for the project. The natural skeptical response to "we have a theory that includes its own praxis" is "lots of people have claimed that; most of them were wrong." The honest reply is: yes, they were, and we know why — the mathematical depth was missing. The mathematics is now available; whether the project succeeds is contingent on whether we deploy it correctly, but the previous failures are not evidence against the attempt being meaningful, because the previous attempts had structurally worse tools.

## Chu spaces / FCA / information flow — live value model and boundary candidate

Chu spaces over a dualizing object `k` package an object as points, states, and an evaluation/incidence pairing. Their duality swaps points and states, making process/context reversal intrinsic rather than an external operation. This is why Chu has real appeal for the project: it speaks directly to the Care axis, formal concept analysis (extension/intension), Barwise-Seligman information flow, and the cobordism-style cup/cap dictionary.

**Relation to our arc.** Chu is not the R4 bridge shape; Benton/LNL remains the bridge between Set-valued locality and V-valued linear/session sheaves. But Chu is now a live value model: `Chu(SET,Bool)` supplies the concrete coefficient instance for the point-generated R4 theorem, and the Sessions-to-Chu `J0`/`J1` realization validates the session object/morphism syntax in that semantic setting. So Chu has moved from "deferred candidate" to "implemented coefficient/value model, with further uses still candidate."

**Watch for.** Places where an object naturally decomposes into extension/intension, points/states, observations/situations, or process/context; places where the Care involution needs to be internal to objects rather than a separate operation; information-flow constructions that look like incidence pairings. These are Chu signals. The implemented Chu stack makes such readings technically available, but the guardrail remains: do not confuse the coefficient/value model with full session operational semantics, O1-in-Chu, or a Reality-reconstruction theorem.

**Reality / Documentation reading.** The Reality discussion re-derived the Chu motivation from another angle: Reality-points, Documentation-states/tests, and an evaluation into `k`. This is better read as Isbell/Gelfand/Chu space-observables duality than holography. The round trip is intentionally lossy: Documentation can preserve the protocol kernel under its tests without reconstructing Reality. Whether the outer Reality/Documentation dualizing object `k` coincides with the inner session dualizing object remains an open conjecture.

## Team semantics — dependence across families of episodes

**Source and status.** The miscellaneous notes'
[team-semantics discussion](arc/xx_misc_notes.md#team-semantics)
(beginning "Team semantics instead evaluates formulas over a set of
assignments") develops a lead already listed in the inventory below.
[Väänänen's research overview](https://www.mv.helsinki.fi/home/jvaanane/teamdep)
describes the established semantics: formulas are evaluated over families of
assignments, allowing dependence to concern their joint variation. The
following no-leak application is Codex's proposal, not a checked result or an
application already constructed in the notes.

**Proposed responsibility.** Distinguish validity of each episode from a
property of a family of admissible episodes. For a deterministic system, let
l be public input, h protected input, and o public output. The dependence atom
=(l,o) requires any two assignments agreeing on l to agree on o. Testing it
across the intended variations of h can express a deterministic noninterference
condition. A family that omits relevant variations proves too little;
nondeterministic systems need a separately justified condition.

A finite comparison uses two runs with the same l and h = 0 or 1. The output
rule o = 0 satisfies the dependence condition; o = h violates it. Both runs can
individually satisfy the same local well-formedness predicate. The example
therefore distinguishes local acceptance from a relational information boundary
without choosing probabilities.

A mathematical team is not automatically a community of participants.
Functional dependence, causal-parent edges, and probabilistic independence are
different notions. Relating the toy example to trusted Hoapa observations is
additional work; it neither extends Tier A by itself nor changes the 2S work
order. See the bounded candidate in future_work.md §E.
