### 1.1 Preliminary insight

In 'The Little Prince' Antoine de Saint-Exupéry shows he thinks like a good engineer, with the same kind of foundational abstractions that Linux, HTTP/REST or Erlang are based on, when he envisions tiny planets with one occupant, for whom everybody else is of the same type. On the asteroid 325 there is only a king, and to this king, everybody else is a subject: "«Ah! Here is a subject,» exclaimed the king, when he saw the little prince coming" 

In Linux, everything is a file: folders are files that list other files, programs are executable files, etc. In HTTP, everything is a resource that you GET, POST, DELETE and so on, and in Erlang, the programming language of choice for telecom, everything is a concurrent process (just like you will be, if you ever join a Hoapa protocol).
