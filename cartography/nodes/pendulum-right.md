The pole of creativity: the pull toward genesis, the problem, the not-yet-formed, the generative source that exceeds current law. It is reached from the anchor by [pendulum-problematization], held in tension with [pendulum-left] via [pendulum-poles-polemic], and discharged toward the working plane by [pendulum-stabilization]. Read as Deleuze in philosophy and Lautman in mathematics; in the logic instance it is the "demand for novelty" that a genetic logic expresses.
