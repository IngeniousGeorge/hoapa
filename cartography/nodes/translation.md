Translation: a claim valid in one context can be transported to another only through an explicit translation preserving specified structure — "no context-smuggling". It is essential for interdisciplinary inquiry, where physical, economic, biological, and social languages cannot be casually interchanged without a typed bridge. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it is the theorem that makes cross-context reuse honest, and it is the natural home of a future `translates-to` edge drawn between two specific domain contexts once those are nodes.
