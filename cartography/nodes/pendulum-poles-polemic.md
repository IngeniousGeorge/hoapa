The paired tension between the two poles — form against novelty, law against genesis — that keeps neither able to absorb the other. It is not resolved at the pole-axis but only displaced into the orthogonal working plane; this productive antagonism is precisely what the equilibrium exists to metabolise. Each instance realises it as the left↔right edge (Hegel↔Deleuze, Lawvere↔Lautman), carrying `relation: pendulum-poles-polemic`.
