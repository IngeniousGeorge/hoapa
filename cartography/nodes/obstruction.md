Explicit obstruction: when local claims cannot be glued into a global one, the system produces an explicit obstruction object rather than failing silently — failed synthesis becomes typed knowledge. This is the constructive reading of contextuality (Abramsky) and descent-failure (Grothendieck): an impossibility is recorded as a thing that can be reasoned about and carried forward. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it supplies exactly what a legitimate context-extension must resolve, which is why [creativity-admissibility] depends on it.
