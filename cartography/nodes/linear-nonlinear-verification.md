The reading of the exponential "!" (the "bang" modality) as verification, modelled by a Benton linear/non-linear adjunction F ⊣ G between a cartesian world and a linear one: G archives (a linear resource becomes a reusable value), F deploys (a reusable value re-enters linear use), and ! = GF is the comonad whose coalgebras are the freely-reusable, verified objects — a "passage to a reusable form". We take it from `workflow/arc/13_catalysts_and_care.md`. Crucially it is an adapter *internal to the concurrency leg*, a discipline on how linear traces become reusable; the arc's grounding pass insists it must not be conflated with the cross-site adjunction (nor "!" with a buffer, nor this passage with descent) — see the `must-not-glue-to` edge to [cross-site-adjunction].
