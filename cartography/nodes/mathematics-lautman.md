Lautman read at the right pole as the demand for novelty: a dialectic of Ideas in which problems and their tensions govern the solutions that mathematics invents — the genetic side where the not-yet-formed problem is primary and structure is its precipitate. His two movements, saturation and liberation (the `saturates`/`liberates` edge-type takes its name here), are the characteristic gestures of this pole. Read with Zalamea as commentator. Held in tension with [mathematics-lawvere] by [pendulum-poles-polemic] and discharged toward the working plane by [pendulum-stabilization].
