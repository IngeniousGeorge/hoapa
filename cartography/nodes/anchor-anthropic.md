The reading of the counit of [cross-site-adjunction] as non-invertible: the passage
from the running community back to pure perspective loses information and cannot be
undone, which is where "the human" — the contingent, the situated — enters and
cannot be abstracted away. Its irreversibility is a feature: it blocks a God's-eye
collapse, aligning with the refusal of any total self-view (see [lawvere-diagonal]).
This is one member of the [anchors] region.
