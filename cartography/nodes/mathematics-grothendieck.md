Grothendieck read at the equilibrium as the cared-for working balance: the "rising sea" method in which one does not force a problem but patiently builds the right context — the topos, the scheme, the relative point of view — until the problem dissolves of itself. This is the mathematical exemplar of the pendulum's orthogonal working plane: the polemic between Lawvere's form and Lautman's problem is metabolised into an ongoing practice of context-construction held with care, not resolved on the pole-axis. It ties directly to the framework's own trace-mathematics and to [recursive-context-articulation] via [pendulum-recontextualization], where the built context becomes the ground of the next problem.
