Deleuze read at the right pole as the demand for novelty: affirmative difference, the virtual, and the genesis that exceeds any settled law — the pole where a problem generates its own solutions rather than receiving form from above. Localized complication (bracketed by the shape, kept here): in the *Logique du sens*, sense is a *surface* effect, not a depth-genesis, so reading Deleuze as pure generative depth flattens the surface/depth apparatus that lets sense be produced without being grounded. The pendulum reads him only through the novelty-demand and forgets this; noted here so the reading does not overclaim, and without adding any edge to the shape.
