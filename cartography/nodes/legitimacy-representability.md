The reading of legitimacy as earned universality: a contribution or authority is legitimate when it is representable — determined by the total pattern of its relations rather than by an intrinsic privilege — which in the enriching setting coincides with reflexive (Isbell) completeness. Its two concrete grounds are "no-leak" (renaming-invariance) and initiality [initiality-bootstrap], so legitimacy is produced, not granted. It is a member of the [praxis-nexus] region and is an instance of the property named in [enriched-completion-setting]. See `workflow/arc/14_trust_and_legitimacy_as_enrichment.md`.
