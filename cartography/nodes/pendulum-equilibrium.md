Not a midpoint between the poles but an orthogonal working plane where their polemic is metabolised into an ongoing practice held in balance with care. It receives [pendulum-verification] from [pendulum-left] and [pendulum-stabilization] from [pendulum-right], and its closing move [pendulum-recontextualization] returns a new anchor to begin the next swing — making the whole shape recursive rather than terminal. Read as Peirce in philosophy and Grothendieck in mathematics; in the logic instance it is [workilibrium-logic] itself, the framework read as its own equilibrium.
