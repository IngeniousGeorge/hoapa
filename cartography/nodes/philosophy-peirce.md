Peirce read at the equilibrium as the cared-for working balance: triadic semiosis as an ongoing interpretive practice, fallibilism, the community of inquiry held together by agape — the "anti-managerial" care that lets independent inquirers stay independent (cf. [anchor-care]). This equilibrium plane is orthogonal to the Hegel–Deleuze pole-axis; it metabolises their polemic into a continuing practice rather than splitting the difference. Two neighbours the four positions do not name co-occupy this plane and are absorbed here rather than lost: Whitehead's process (reality as becoming-in-practice) and Simondon's individuation (the resolution of tensions into metastable structure). Its closing move [pendulum-recontextualization] returns a fresh anchor, which in the framework is [recursive-context-articulation].
