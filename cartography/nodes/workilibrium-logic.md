A coined term (work + equilibrium) for the equilibrium position of the *logic* pendulum — and the single drawn member of a logic instance we have otherwise only seeded. Its firm point: the equilibrium of the logic pendulum is the Hoapa framework itself, read as its own cared-for working balance. The governing dyad is "return to equilibrium" (the context side — a milieu that must account for itself and be brought back into balance) crossed with "optimality of care" (the multiplicity side — [anchor-care]'s anti-managerial affect, which keeps independent inquirers independent). Work is primary: the equilibrium is something done and maintained, not a state inherited — trace and inheritance are *consequences* of the work, never its definition (reducing it to inheritance is degenerate, the way a parent who optimises for the bequest is a bad parent). The tentative anchor of this logic pendulum is "the immanent context" (the milieu with no outside judge); its poles fall out as the demand for form (internal / formal logic, [pendulum-left]) and the demand for novelty (genetic logic, [pendulum-right]). It concretely fills [pendulum-equilibrium] in the logic domain. The full logic instance and its forcing-colimit (the retained [logic-neighbour-colimit]) are left for later; workilibrium is the equilibrium seed around which they will be drawn — and note that the `forced-by` derivational edge must stay at that colimit level, not on this node alone.
