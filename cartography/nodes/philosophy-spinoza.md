Spinoza read as the philosophy pendulum's immanent ground: substance as immanent cause, a Nature that is its own explanation with no transcendent outside to judge it. This is why he anchors the shape — the pendulum's whole "no outside view" commitment is Spinozist immanence made into a starting milieu whose own tensions drive the swing toward form and toward novelty. The reading takes only this immanence-as-ground aspect; Spinoza's own necessitarianism and parallelism are left aside as not load-bearing for the position. [pendulum-formalization] carries this anchor toward [philosophy-hegel], [pendulum-problematization] toward [philosophy-deleuze].
