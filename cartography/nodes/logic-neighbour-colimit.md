A forcing-colimit, not a shape: the diagram of established positions whose shared shape forces the framework's notion of logic, [logic-as-contextual-transformation]. Dewey (logic as inquiry), Girard (linear/resource logic), Lawvere (internal logic of a topos), Martin-Löf (constructive inhabitation), Abramsky (contextuality, failed gluing), Pym–O'Hearn (bunched/separation logic, non-collapsing local work), and Goguen–Burstall institution theory (networks of logics and their translations). It is the inner-engine counterpart to [philosophy-neighbour-colimit]: no single author states the whole, but each fixes one facet of "valid transformation under context", and the [logic-as-contextual-transformation] `forced-by` edge into this node carries that derivation. The logic pendulum's equilibrium seed [workilibrium-logic] is *not* this colimit: per "shapes vs forcing-colimits" the forcing stays here, at the colimit level, even once the logic pendulum is drawn — never migrating onto the equilibrium node alone.
