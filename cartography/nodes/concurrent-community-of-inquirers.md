The community side: a Peircean Community of Inquirers realised as a concurrent actor
system in which all inquirers share one type ("unityped actors") and only the traces
of their interaction are richly typed — the design law is "type moves, not people".
It draws on Peirce's community-of-inquiry and agapism, Hewitt's actor model, and
session-typed concurrency (Caires–Pfenning). Verification is mechanised (proof
assistants as a public discharge organ) so that no human class monopolises checking,
and the rules are themselves editable through the same typed traces. It supplies the
right-hand site of [cross-site-adjunction] and is the locus of the `practice`
perspective. See `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md` and
`workflow/deep_context/concurrency_anchor.md`.
