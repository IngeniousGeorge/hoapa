The position an inquiry departs from and must ultimately account for without appeal to any outside judge: the immanent context or milieu taken as given at the start of a swing. It is not a foundation in the justificatory sense but the concrete situation whose own tensions drive the motion toward the two poles. [pendulum-formalization] carries it toward [pendulum-left] and [pendulum-problematization] toward [pendulum-right]; [pendulum-recontextualization] returns a fresh anchor to this position from [pendulum-equilibrium], closing the loop. Read as Spinoza in the philosophy instance and Riemann in the mathematics instance.
