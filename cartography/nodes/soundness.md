Soundness: nothing is accepted as formally verified unless there exists a checkable proof object for it under explicit assumptions — "accepted" never means "someone said so". It is the base guarantee of the [verification-layer] and the floor on which every other member of the [protocol-theorems] family stands, since provenance, auditability, and the anti-privilege results all assume that acceptance already tracks a real proof. Stated in `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
