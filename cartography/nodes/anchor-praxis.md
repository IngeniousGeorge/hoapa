The reading of the unit of [cross-site-adjunction] as praxis: the natural
transformation by which a context enters its own running realisation and returns
changed. It is the categorical home, at the level of the whole framework, of the
"borrow – work – give back" cycle named in [logic-as-contextual-transformation].
This is one member of the [anchors] region.
