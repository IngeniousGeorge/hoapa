The incubator is a concrete incarnation of the initiality bootstrap: the place that produces new inquirers and contexts is the free/initial construction that produces a secure base rather than presupposing one. So "where the new comes from" and "how the framework starts without a foundation" are the same self-stabilising move — which is what lets the incubator be a birthplace and a bootstrap at once, and ties the ethical reading (care's fixed point) to the foundational one.
