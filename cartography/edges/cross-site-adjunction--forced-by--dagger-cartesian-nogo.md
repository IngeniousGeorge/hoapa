Why the framework has two legs at all is forced by the no-go theorem: since a nontrivial dagger-compact (self-dual, conserved, non-copyable) category cannot be cartesian (copyable), the linear concurrency side and the cartesian perspectival side cannot be one category, so their relation has to be a bridge between two — an adjunction — rather than a structure inside one. The separation of concerns is thereby a theorem, not a taste, and [cross-site-adjunction] is its necessary shape; this complements the other forcing of the adjunction, its directionality, by [lawvere-diagonal].
