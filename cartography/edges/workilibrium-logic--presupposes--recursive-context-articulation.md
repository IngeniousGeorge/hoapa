The "return to equilibrium" half of [workilibrium-logic]'s dyad *is* the recursion of [recursive-context-articulation]: the milieu accounting for itself and being handed on as the next context. Workilibrium cannot stand without that recursive articulation — it is the equilibrium of exactly that motion, read on the logic pendulum, and the pendulum's own [pendulum-recontextualization] loop is the same move.
