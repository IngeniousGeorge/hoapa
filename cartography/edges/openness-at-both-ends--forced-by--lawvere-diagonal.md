The refusal of any universal container — inner (no type of all types) or outer (no
totality of all contexts) — is forced by the diagonal argument: total
self-parametrisation together with a fixed-point-free operation is contradictory.
The universe hierarchy on the inner end and the never-enclosed exterior on the outer
end are thus not two separate design choices but the same impossibility seen from two
sides, both consequences of [lawvere-diagonal].
