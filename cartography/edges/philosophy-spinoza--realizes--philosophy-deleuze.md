Realises [pendulum-problematization] in the philosophy instance: the swing from Spinozist immanence to the Deleuzian demand for novelty. The same immanent ground, pressed on its other side, yields not form but a generative problem — affirmative difference as the excess immanence carries within it. The edge is the concrete form of the anchor→right invariant relation, and Deleuze's own debt to Spinoza's immanence makes the swing especially tight.
