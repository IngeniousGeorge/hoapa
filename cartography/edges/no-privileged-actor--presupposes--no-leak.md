An individualised member edge: the no-privileged-actor theorem presupposes no-leak because equal treatment of equivalent moves can only hold if the system cannot tell whose move it is except through the typed contribution. Were identity to leak, two identical certificates could be treated differently by their authors' status — exactly what this theorem forbids — so [no-leak] is the condition that makes the anti-privilege guarantee provable rather than merely declared.
