Catalytic factorization is a clean instance of "borrow – work – give back" in which the borrowed thing is a catalyst returned unchanged: the explorer is borrowed and given back, the territory does the work of becoming a chart, and nothing about the explorer is consumed. This makes it the practice-facing picture of the same logic that Noether makes physics-facing and Gentzen makes proof-facing — the explorer plays the role of the discharged assumption, conserved across the transformation.
