An internal region edge: creativity-admissibility presupposes the obstruction theorem, because its admissibility criterion refers to "resolving a typed obstruction" — and it is [obstruction] that guarantees a failed gluing has been recorded as an explicit, reasonable-about object in the first place. So the two members interlock: obstruction turns failure into typed knowledge, and creativity-admissibility licenses a context-extension exactly when it discharges such an object.
