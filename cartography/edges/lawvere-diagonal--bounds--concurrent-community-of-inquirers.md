Lawvere's diagonal result bounds the community from within: a self-editing system
rich enough to represent its own verifications cannot hold a complete,
classically-negating account of its own truth. So the trace layer can be autonomous
but never closed — it must stay stratified, modal, and revisable, and, crucially, no
human may occupy the missing total meta-position (that is the anti-managerial
payoff). The impossibility is productive: each diagonal obstruction forces a lift to
a new context, which is the very engine of [recursive-context-articulation].
