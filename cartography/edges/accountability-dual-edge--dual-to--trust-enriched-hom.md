Accountability is the *-dual back-edge to trust: every forward hom carrying a commitment has a return edge along which responsibility can be traced, so the two are related by the setting's star-duality. Whether this is only a duality (an involution) or in fact a full adjunction (with unit and counit) is an open fork from `workflow/arc/14_trust_and_legitimacy_as_enrichment.md`; recording it as `dual-to` takes the conservative reading and leaves the adjunction upgrade as a marked question for a later edge.
