An individualised member edge: the flagship trace-sufficiency theorem presupposes no-leak, since "every authority-function factors through the typed trace" can only be true if nothing about authority lives outside the trace in the form of leaked identity. [no-leak] is precisely what guarantees the trace carries the whole of authority, so it is the semantic core the flagship formalises and the reason the person can be dispensed with in favour of the work.
