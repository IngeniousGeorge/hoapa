The proposition is not posited freely; it is forced as the shared shape — colimit, "dépassement" — of the neighbour diagram [philosophy-neighbour-colimit]. Each neighbour isolates one aspect (Hegelian mediation, Deleuzian difference, Whiteheadian process, Peircean semiosis, Lautmanian saturation/liberation), and the proposition is precisely what lets all of them map in without collapsing into one another. This edge carries the graph's "why it is convincing": the shape is derived from a standing diagram of established positions, not chosen for taste. The [philosophy] pendulum enriches this colimit with reading-structure but does not replace this forcing.
