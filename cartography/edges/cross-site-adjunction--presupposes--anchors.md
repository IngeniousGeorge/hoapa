A region-targeted (mutualised) edge: the adjunction becomes a specific claim, rather
than a formal analogy, only once all five anchors are in place — a left adjoint
(complexity), a right adjoint (evolution), a unit (praxis), a counit (the anthropic
non-invertibility), and the transverse involution (care). Remove any one and the
object under conjecture loses its determinacy. Because the target is the [anchors]
region, this presupposition distributes to every member unless a member carries an
individual edge that overrides it.
