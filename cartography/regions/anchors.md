The five commitments that pin [cross-site-adjunction] down to a specific adjunction
rather than a formal analogy. Four locate its categorical parts — a left adjoint
[anchor-complexity], a right adjoint [anchor-evolution], a unit [anchor-praxis], a
counit [anchor-anthropic]; the fifth [anchor-care] is a transverse order-two
structure. They share most of their external edges — each refines the same
adjunction — but each also carries individual edges, and the region has internal
structure. Treat the region as semi-autonomous: an edge whose target is `anchors`
distributes to all five members unless a member individually overrides it.
