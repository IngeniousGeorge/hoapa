The enriched-completion setting presupposes its base: the quantale / *-autonomous structure and its Isbell completion are built over the linear, conserved base that [risk-linear-base] supplies. Without a resource-sensitive base there would be no meaningful notion of "cost", and the completion — hence representability, hence legitimacy — would collapse into a trivial, cost-free structure. So risk is upstream of the whole formal home of the praxis nexus.
