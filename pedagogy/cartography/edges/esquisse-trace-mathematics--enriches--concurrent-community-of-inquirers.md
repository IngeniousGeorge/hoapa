The Esquisse toolkit enriches the community's trace layer: raw interaction records become a sheaf/stack over a site of contexts, with typed moves as cells, forks and merges as descent problems, and presentation-invariance (relabelling, actor anonymity) as the groupoid symmetry acted on. This is the conjectural proposal for the mathematics of the verification layer — motivated by the local-to-global, invariance, and gluing demands that layer must meet — and it is what would let a trace be small enough to inspect yet deep enough to carry a world.
