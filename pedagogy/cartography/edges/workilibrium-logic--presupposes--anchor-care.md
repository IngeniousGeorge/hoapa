The "optimality of care" half of [workilibrium-logic]'s dyad is [anchor-care]: the order-two, anti-managerial affect that keeps independent inquirers independent while remaining open to common truth. Workilibrium presupposes it — without care the equilibrium degenerates into managed inheritance, the very reduction (a parent optimising for the bequest) the concept explicitly rejects.
