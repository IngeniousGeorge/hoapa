Noether conservation is the field-theoretic instance of "borrow – work – give back": varying the fields in a bounded region is the borrowing, the symmetry of the action is the work, and the conserved current through the boundary is the discharge — the accounting that shows nothing was extracted without return. This is what makes the framework's logic literal rather than figurative in physics, and it is the technical seam through which [cobordism-field-theories] connects back to the framework's account of valid transformation.
