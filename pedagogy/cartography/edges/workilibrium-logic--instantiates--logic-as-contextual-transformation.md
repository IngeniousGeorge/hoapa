[workilibrium-logic] is a reflexive realization of [logic-as-contextual-transformation]: the framework read as its own cared-for equilibrium is one concrete case of "valid transformation under context" — the context being the framework's own practice. It instantiates rather than replaces the general notion; the notion of logic stays broader than this single equilibrium reading, which is why the derivational `forced-by` edge remains on [logic-neighbour-colimit] and not here.
