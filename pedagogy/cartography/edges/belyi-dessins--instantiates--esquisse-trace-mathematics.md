Belyi's theorem with its dessins is the paradigmatic instance of the Esquisse trace-mathematics: a finite combinatorial trace — a bipartite graph on a surface — that faithfully carries a rich hidden world (an arithmetic curve with its Galois action), reconstructed via exactly the sites/descent/moduli machinery the constellation names. It is the concrete picture behind the otherwise abstract claim that small public traces can encode and regenerate global structure.
