The field-theory ladder presupposes the conservation discipline: a cobordism only counts as a legitimate transformation between boundary-contexts when the work across it is accounted for by conserved currents and boundary charges, and an anomaly (a failed conservation) is exactly what obstructs an otherwise-available theory. So [noether-conservation] is the admissibility condition on the transformations that [cobordism-field-theories] composes.
