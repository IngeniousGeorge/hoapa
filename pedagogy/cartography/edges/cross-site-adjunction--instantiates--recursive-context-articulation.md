The adjunction is the framework's concrete realisation of the abstract recurring
shape: "context → multiplicity → language-type → new context" becomes
"perspective-topos —Allow→ running community —Require→ perspective-topos". The unit
and counit of the adjunction are the formal home of the recursion's forward and
return legs. This edge is where the harmony thesis becomes checkable: the
metaphysics is not decoration laid over the mathematics — the mathematics is an
instance of the metaphysics, so the same shape can be pointed to on both sides.
