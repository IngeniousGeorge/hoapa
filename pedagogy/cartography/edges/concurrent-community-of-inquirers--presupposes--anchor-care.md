An individualised member edge — an addition beyond the mutualised edges the [anchors]
region already receives: the community leg specifically presupposes care/agape as its
enabling condition. Without the order-two "let differences stay independent"
involution, the unityped community degenerates into either hierarchy (a managerial
class recaptures verification) or unaccountable proliferation. This encodes one of the
framework's temporal inversions: care sits *upstream* of the verification machinery,
not as an ethical gloss appended after it.
