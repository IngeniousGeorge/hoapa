Creativity is forced into existence by the diagonal obstruction: when a context rich enough to speak about itself cannot totally classify itself, the resulting fixed-point/obstruction cannot be resolved inside the current logic and demands a new one — a lift to a higher universe, a new modality, a new type. So [lawvere-diagonal] is not only the anti-sovereignty bound on the community but the reason creativity is structurally necessary rather than optional: the impossibility of self-closure is the engine of context-change.
