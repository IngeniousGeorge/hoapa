Realises [pendulum-poles-polemic] in the philosophy instance: the paired, symmetric tension between Hegel's form and Deleuze's novelty — the well-known polemic of mediation-through-negation against affirmative difference. Deleuze's explicit anti-Hegelianism is the sharpest instance of the invariant relation: neither pole absorbs the other, and the antagonism is only displaced, not resolved, until the orthogonal equilibrium metabolises it. Being paired, this edge is read as symmetric (order not load-bearing).
