Two Isbell faces of the framework meet here: the bulk/boundary duality (a space against its documentation) and the reflexive (Isbell) completion of the praxis nexus (the foreign treated as one's own) are the same reflexive-completion machinery read on different objects. Under the perspective-flip, "chart reality without enclosing it" and "earn legitimacy by becoming representable" are dual readings of Isbell completeness — which is why this `dual-to` links the reality side and the practice side through one piece of mathematics.
