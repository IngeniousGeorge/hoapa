The coordinate reading cannot stand on its own. "The number assigned to a participant is arbitrary" is, before the theorem, a design hope — the sort of thing a system believes about itself until someone checks. What licenses it is the proved invariance: since the verdict is unchanged along every non-merging relabelling, and in particular along every reversible one, the assignment genuinely carries no information the checker can act on. Note the direction of support, because the project's anti-drift notes insist on it: the mathematics licenses the reading, never the reverse. Arguing from the gauge picture to a definitional choice in the Agda would invert a one-way relation and was caught doing so once already during the session that produced these records.
