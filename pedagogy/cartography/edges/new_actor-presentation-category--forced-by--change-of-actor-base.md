The category is not reached for; it is the resolution of a difficulty already met in code. Interpreting a record into the substrate builds a participant carrier from that record's own declared participants, so a record and its relabelling determine carriers of different types, while the substrate's morphisms fix one carrier and carry no participant component — leaving the comparison inexpressible rather than merely awkward. Naming a base whose objects are carriers and whose arrows are non-merging relabellings dissolves that: the comparison becomes a morphism over an arrow of the base, and the hypothesis that had to be threaded through every lemma becomes a property the arrows already have. This is the discipline the project demands before generalising — name the concrete case that forced the definition — and it is why an abstract wrapper over relabellings, proposed and rejected during the same session, would have floated nothing.
