Lawvere read at the left pole as the demand for form: the drive to internalise logic and structure categorically — adjoint functors as a precise "unity of opposites", the elementary topos as a world with its own internal logic, cohesion axiomatised as adjunctions. He answers the intrinsic-geometry anchor by giving it universal, structural law. This is the same figure whose adjoint-unity was a neighbour of the metaphysics diagram; in the pendulum he sits cleanly at the mathematical form-pole, in productive tension with [mathematics-lautman] via [pendulum-poles-polemic] and discharged toward the working plane by [pendulum-verification].
