Riemann read as the mathematics pendulum's immanent ground: the manifold specified by intrinsic local data — a metric given from within — with no ambient space to embed in or judge from. This is geometry that accounts for itself locally, the mathematical face of the same immanence Spinoza anchors in philosophy: the situation carries its own structure, and its tensions (curvature, singularity, the need for global coherence) drive the swing toward form and toward problem.
