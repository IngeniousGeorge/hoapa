The pole of intelligibility: the pull toward internal logic, law, settled structure, the already-formed — the side on which a situation is answered by being given form. It is reached from the anchor by [pendulum-formalization], held in irreducible tension with [pendulum-right] by [pendulum-poles-polemic], and discharged toward the working plane by [pendulum-verification]. Read as Hegel in philosophy and Lawvere in mathematics; in the logic instance it is the "demand for form" that the internal / formal logic expresses.
