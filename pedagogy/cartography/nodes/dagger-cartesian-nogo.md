The no-go theorem that a nontrivial dagger-compact category cannot be cartesian, used here as a forcing constraint: it means the framework's two sides cannot be collapsed into one, because a self-dual, linear, conserved, non-copyable regime (the concurrency / process side) is provably incompatible with a cartesian, intuitionistic, copyable, directed regime (the perspectival type-theory side). So the separation of concerns is not a design preference but a theorem — you cannot have both free copyability and genuinely conserved, non-copyable resources in one category. Drawn from `workflow/arc/13_catalysts_and_care.md`; it is why [cross-site-adjunction] must bridge two different categories rather than live inside one, and why the conserved base of the praxis nexus can exist at all.
