A deliberately provisional seed for a third instance of the [pendulum] shape, drawn over natural science and flagged as blurry — not yet earned. The tentative casting: an anchor of empirical immanence (Bacon–Galileo, the situation interrogated from within), a left pole of the demand for form (Newton–Einstein, law and unifying theory), a right pole of the demand for novelty (Darwin, genetic / evolutionary explanation), and an equilibrium of cared-for inquiry (Peirce again, the community of inquiry and fallibilist method — a distinct reading-node from [philosophy-peirce], since positions are shape-scoped). Unlike [philosophy] and [mathematics] this reading is unresolved: the pairings — especially compressing Newton and Einstein into one pole, and reusing Peirce across instances — are not settled, so it is recorded as a single seed node rather than built out into positions and edges. Its role is to test whether the shape generalises beyond philosophy and mathematics; if it holds, it becomes a full instance — acquiring a forcing-colimit only if a genuine neighbour-diagram argument later emerges, not as a matter of course.
