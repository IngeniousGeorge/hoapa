Conservative self-editing: a proposed change to the system's own rules either preserves prior verified results under translation, or explicitly marks which results are invalidated, migrated, or made context-bound. It is what lets the community edit itself without silently destroying its past, and so it is the theorem that makes reflexive self-governance safe rather than reckless. A member of the [protocol-theorems] region, it is the condition under which the community's self-editability — a defining feature of the concurrent leg — does not become a way to erase inconvenient history.
