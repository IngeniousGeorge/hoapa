The point where three threads of the construction turn out to coincide: the "incubator" — the place where new inquirers and new contexts are brought into being — is at once the fixed point of the care involution [anchor-care], a "points" construction from [chu-isbell-duality], and the initiality / free-model bootstrap [initiality-bootstrap]. That these three are one object is one of the arc's convergences (`workflow/arc/13_catalysts_and_care.md`): what appears as an ethical condition (care), a semantic construction (Chu points), and a foundational move (initiality) are three descriptions of a single self-stabilising thing. "Incubator" is our plain-English image for that shared fixed point; its interest is that it makes the framework's ethics, semantics, and foundations meet at one place rather than run in parallel.
