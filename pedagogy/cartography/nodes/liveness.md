Liveness: under fair participation and bounded obligations, every valid proposal eventually reaches a definite status — accepted, rejected, forked, deferred, or obstructed — so the system cannot bury a valid contribution forever. It is what stops managerial delay from reappearing as procedural inertia: where the other members of the [protocol-theorems] region constrain *what* is accepted, liveness constrains *that a decision is reached at all*. A member of the family, it is the safety-versus-progress complement to soundness.
