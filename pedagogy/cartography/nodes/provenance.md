Provenance: every accepted claim has a finite dependency path back to its assumptions, data, definitions, rules, and prior accepted moves — nothing enters the trace without visible ancestry. This is the condition for trust without authority: a contribution is trustworthy because its lineage can be inspected, not because of who made it. It is a member of the [protocol-theorems] region and the ground on which [collective-memory] builds; the audit-layer detail lives in `workflow/arc/10_provenance_audit_layer.md`.
