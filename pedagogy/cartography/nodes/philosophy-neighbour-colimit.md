A forcing-colimit, not a shape: the diagram of established metaphysical positions whose shared shape — "dépassement", in the arc's word — forces [recursive-context-articulation]. Hegel and Deleuze supply the seeding tension (mediation-through-negation versus affirmative difference), joined by Whitehead (process), Peirce (triadic semiosis), Simondon (individuation), Lautman and Zalamea (saturation/liberation as mathematical praxis), and Lawvere (adjoint unity-of-opposites, cohesion). Its job is derivational: the concept is positioned not as a new doctrine but as the shared shape into which each neighbour maps while keeping what matters, so the [recursive-context-articulation] `forced-by` edge into this node carries the graph's "why it is convincing". The [philosophy] pendulum is the *structured realization* of this colimit — it enriches the flat diagram with four positions and six invariant relations — but the two are kept distinct (see the schema's "shapes vs forcing-colimits"): the pendulum gives reading-structure, this node carries the forcing. Its inner-engine counterpart is [logic-neighbour-colimit].
