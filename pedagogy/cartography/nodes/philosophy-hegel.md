Hegel read at the left pole as the demand for form: the labour of the concept, mediation-through-negation, the self-moving determination that answers the immanent ground by giving it intelligible structure. Localized complication (bracketed by the shape, kept here): in the *Science of Logic* the categories are not merely imposed form but *generate themselves* — a genesis-of-categories in which the "form pole" secretes its own content, so Hegel is not purely on the side of the already-formed. The pendulum reads him only through the form-demand and forgets this wrinkle; it is recorded here so the reading stays honest without leaking into the shape's invariant edges.
