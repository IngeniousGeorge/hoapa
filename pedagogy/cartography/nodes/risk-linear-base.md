The reading of risk as the linear, conserved base over which the whole praxis nexus is enriched: because resources cannot be freely duplicated or discarded, every valued interaction is exposure, and risk is not an add-on but the ambient quantity the enrichment measures against. This is why trust and legitimacy have "cost" at all. It is a member of the [praxis-nexus] region and is the base underlying [enriched-completion-setting].
