The framework's outer pole: an exterior — what it calls "reality" — that is never made an internal sort, and is present only negatively, through the disciplines it imposes (unityping, non-enactment, availability-only access, buffered entry). It is the thing the recursion explores and forms perspectives on but never encloses; treating it as an internal object would be the characteristic error the design guards against. It is the outer face of [openness-at-both-ends] (the inner face being the universe hierarchy). See `workflow/arc/11_reality.md`.
