The cluster of tools from Grothendieck's *Esquisse d'un Programme* — Grothendieck topologies / sites, sheaves and stacks, moduli, descent, tame ("moderated") topology, dévissage, and the "principle of anodyne choices" — read as the mathematics of how small local traces reconstruct hidden global structure. We propose it, conjecturally, as the natural architecture for the verification/trace layer of [concurrent-community-of-inquirers], because that layer needs exactly local-to-global assembly, presentation-invariance, and descent. It is a meta-module: a named toolkit rather than a single theorem, gathered in `workflow/arc/15_metaphysics_and_logic.md`.
